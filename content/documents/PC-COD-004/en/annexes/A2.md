P = prepares · D = decides · C = co-signs or signs off · I = is informed · A = prior agreement. CEO = Chief Executive Officer; 2SM = second senior manager; HCM = Head of Capital Markets; HS = Head of Sales; CHB = Chairman of the Board of Directors; BD = Board of Directors. The FCFA thresholds are those in Article 27 and in the drafting notes, to be confirmed by the Board.
