The deputy acts with the powers of the holder, within the same limits. The deputy never controls a task that they perform during the back-up period (Article 25 of the charter).

| Function | Deputy | Second deputy | Powers excluded from the back-up | Compensating control | Deputy's professional card |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chairman of the Board of Directors | Director designated by the Board | Oldest director | Online sign-off in the repository on behalf of the Chairman | Report to the Chairman on return | Not required |
| Chief Executive Officer | Second senior manager | Head of department designated by the Board | Acts reserved to the Board; co-signing an act they prepared | Chairman of the Board informed of any act above FCFA 10,000,000 | Not required |
| Second senior manager | Chief Executive Officer | Head of department designated by the Board | — | Second signature by another signatory | According to the function |
| Head of Capital Markets | Chief Executive Officer | Head of Sales (day-to-day follow-up, without inside information) | Signing mandates; allocations | Allocations validated by the RCCI | To be checked if the back-up lasts more than 30 days |
| Head of Sales | Client relationship officer | Chief Executive Officer | Validation of a high-risk client onboarding | Validation by the RCCI | Required for advice to clients |
| Client relationship officer | Head of Sales | Chief Executive Officer | — | — | Required for advice to clients |
| RCCI and AML/CFT officer | Deputy designated after informing the Chairman of the Board | Specialist service provider | Control of a task performed by the deputy themselves | Review of files by the RCCI on return | Required if the back-up lasts more than 30 days (to be confirmed) |
| Operations officer | Chief Executive Officer | Second senior manager | Signing payments they prepared | After-the-fact control of reconciliations by the RCCI | To be confirmed |
| Accountant | Accounting firm | — | Bank signature | Reconciliation reviewed by the Chief Executive Officer | Not required |
| IT service provider | Back-up provider (PC-POL-PCA-001) | — | Permanent administrator rights | Access review by the RCCI | Not required |
