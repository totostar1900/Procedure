nature: "Form"
renseigne_par: "Any Staff Member, then the RCCI"
conserve_par: "RCCI (incident file, single register) · ten years"
frequence: "On the day of discovery, at the latest within 24 hours"
---

Report what you know, even if you have not understood everything or the error has already been corrected. An error reported in good faith is not sanctioned; only concealment is. If the incident is ongoing or appears serious, first call the RCCI and the Chief Executive Officer.

**Never describe here a suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing**: use the reporting form of the AML/CFT Policy (PC-POL-LBC-001, Appendix 4).
