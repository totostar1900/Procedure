**1. Severity.** One criterion is enough. Where in doubt, choose the higher level.

| Criterion | Minor | Significant | Major |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clients | No client affected, or error corrected the same day with no effect on the client | One client affected: execution error, late payment, inaccurate information | Loss for several clients; unexplained difference on client assets |
| Financial loss (Company or clients) | Less than FCFA 1,000,000 | From FCFA 1,000,000 to less than FCFA 10,000,000 | FCFA 10,000,000 or more, or loss bringing own funds closer to their alert threshold |
| Regulation | Departure from an internal procedure, without regulatory breach | Regulatory breach: late submission to COSUMAF, incomplete client file, mandatory control not performed | Breach liable to a COSUMAF sanction or to call the licence into question; proven fraud |
| Systems and continuity | Brief outage with no effect on clients | Interruption of a service for more than half a day; data compromised without proven disclosure | Successful cyberattack; breach of client data; interruption of services to clients for more than one business day |
| Reputation | No external effect | Dissatisfaction of a client or partner | Public criticism of the Company; incident involving a senior manager |
| Repetition and near miss | Near miss without serious potential | Repeated minor incident; near miss that would have been major | Repeated significant incident |

Control findings are rated in accordance with Article 16 of the Internal Control Charter (PC-COD-003); a COSUMAF finding or injunction is rated at least significant.

**2. Escalation and time limits.**

| Step | Minor | Significant | Major |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Report to the RCCI | Same day, at the latest 24 hours | Same day, at the latest 24 hours | Immediately by telephone, then form the same day |
| Classification | Following business day | Following business day | Immediately |
| Chief Executive Officer | Monthly review | Within 24 hours | Immediately |
| Chairman of the Board of Directors | Quarterly report | Within 24 hours | Immediately; written note the same day |
| Directors | Quarterly report | Quarterly report | Within 5 business days |
| Notifications (COSUMAF, data protection authority, BVMAC, insurer) | No | To be examined | To be examined the same day |
| Root-cause analysis and action plan | Only if repeated | Within 10 business days | Within 5 business days |
| Approval of the plan | RCCI | Chief Executive Officer | Chief Executive Officer; Chairman of the Board informed |
| Correction | Within 6 months | Within 3 months | Within 1 month |
| Verification of effectiveness | No | About 3 months after completion | About 3 months after completion |
