**The five whys.** Start from the fact and ask "why?" until reaching a cause on which the Company can act. Each answer is based on a verified fact. Do not stop at "human error": ask why the framework did not stop the error.

> **Example.** A buy order was executed on another client's account. *Why?* The client relationship officer entered a wrong account number. *Why?* Two clients have the same name and entry is made by name. *Why?* The tool lists clients by name, without showing the account number or the date of birth. *Why?* This case had not been anticipated in the configuration. **Root cause**: tool. **Actions**: show the account number and a second identifier at entry (corrective); check the other client selection screens (preventive); remind the team of the check that the signed order matches the transmitted order (corrective).
