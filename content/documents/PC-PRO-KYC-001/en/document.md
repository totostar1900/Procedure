## Title I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

This procedure describes, step by step, how Purpose Capital S.A. (the "Company") onboards a client: what must be requested, verified and decided, who does it, within what time limit, and what must be kept as evidence. It also describes how the file is updated during the relationship and how it is closed.

It implements the AML/CFT Policy (PC-POL-LBC-001) and the Code of Conduct. Where there is doubt, the policy prevails.

### Article 2 — Scope

The procedure applies to all clients, whatever the channel and the product:

- **types of clients**: natural persons, legal persons, groups (tontines, associations, joint ownerships (indivisions)), institutional investors;
- **channels**: the Guichet, the Company's online service, which is the main channel; the branch, on paper, for clients who cannot or do not wish to use the Guichet.

The content of the checks is the same whatever the channel. Only the way they are carried out changes.

### Article 3 — Who does what

| Function | Role in the procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Relationship manager (Sales Department) | Welcomes the client, explains the process, helps them put the file together, collects the documents at the branch |
| Desk (operations) | Processes the file: checks, screening, risk rating; decides for low and standard risk; opens the accounts. This function is performed by the Chief Executive Officer until a head of operations is appointed |
| AML/CFT officer (RCCI) | Reviews standard-risk files within one month; gives a written opinion for high-risk clients and PEPs; handles matches against sanctions lists; monitors the procedure |
| Chief Executive Officer | Decides on onboarding for high-risk clients and PEPs |

The person who collected the file may not be the only person to approve it. Where staffing does not allow this four-eyes check, the RCCI's review within one month takes its place.

### Article 4 — Five rules with no exceptions

1. **No account without an approved file**, and no transaction before the agreement has been accepted.
2. **Nothing is signed before approval**: the client accepts the agreement once their file has been approved, not before.
3. **No cash**: funds come from a bank account or electronic money account in the client's name, and are paid out only to that account.
4. **No third parties**: the Company acts only for the client or for the client's representatives declared in the file.
5. **No tipping off**: a suspicion is never disclosed to the client, whether directly or through the reason given for a refusal.

## Title II — The onboarding steps

### Article 5 — Step 1: first contact

The relationship manager or the Guichet presents the Company, its services, its fees and the account opening process to the client. Before going any further, the relationship manager makes sure:

- that the client is of age and has legal capacity, or is represented by an authorised person;
- that the relationship does not fall within the relationships prohibited by the AML/CFT Policy: shell bank, client refusing to identify themselves, person or country subject to sanctions, anonymous account;
- of the purpose of the relationship: investment, savings, financing of an association, cash management;
- for a joint ownership (indivision), that the amount envisaged does not exceed FCFA 25,000,000 in nominal value; above that amount, the group must incorporate as a registered association.

### Article 6 — Step 2: putting the file together

The client provides the information and documents listed in Appendix 1, according to their type:

- their **identity** and contact details;
- for a legal person or a group, its **representatives**, its **joint holders** and its **beneficial owners**, with the date of birth of each person declared;
- their **bank account** in the client's name (bank account details, RIB), to which all sums due to them will be paid;
- the **source of the funds** they intend to invest and the order of magnitude of the amounts;
- their declaration as to whether they are a **politically exposed person** or connected with such a person;
- their **investor profile** (Appendix 3);
- their **consent** to the processing of their data, after receiving the information required by law.

In the Guichet, the client completes the file themselves and uploads the documents; they can only submit it once it is complete. At the branch, the relationship manager completes the file with the client, checks the originals and makes copies, which the relationship manager dates and signs.

### Article 7 — Step 3: identity verification

The desk checks:

- that the identity document is **valid** on the date of verification and that its details match those in the file;
- at the branch, that the **original** has been seen; the copy bears the words "original seen", the date and the name of the Staff Member;
- remotely, that **two independent verification elements** have been obtained, in accordance with Article 17 of the AML/CFT Policy (Appendix 5 for the video interview); the client is then rated at least standard risk;
- for a resident outside CEMAC, that a video interview has taken place and that a foreign proof of address has been provided;
- that the **account name on the RIB** matches the client's name exactly;
- for a legal person, that the registration, articles of association and powers are up to date and consistent.

A document that is doubtful, illegible, expired or inconsistent is refused. A document that appears to be forged is reported to the RCCI, without informing the client.

### Article 8 — Step 4: representatives and beneficial owners

For a legal person or a group, the desk:

- identifies every natural person who holds, directly or indirectly, more than 25% of the capital or voting rights, or who exercises control by other means; failing that, the principal senior executive;
- traces successive holdings up to the natural persons where the shareholder is itself a company;
- verifies the identity of the representatives and of the persons authorised to give orders, and the group's decision-making rule;
- checks that each person declared has a date of birth in the file: without it, screening cannot rule out a namesake.

### Article 9 — Step 5: sanctions and PEP screening

The desk screens the client, their representatives and their beneficial owners:

- against the sanctions lists applicable in CEMAC (United Nations Security Council lists and national lists) and, for information, against those of the European Union and the United States;
- against available PEP databases and open sources (name search and adverse information).

In the Guichet, an automatic pre-check suggests possible matches; it does not replace the **desk's attestation**, which states the lists consulted, the date and the result: no match, documented false positive, or confirmed match.

In the event of a **confirmed match** or serious doubt regarding a sanctions list, the desk stops processing the file and immediately informs the RCCI, who applies the AML/CFT Policy (freezing, reporting to the authorities). The client is not informed.

### Article 10 — Step 6: client profile and category

The client answers the questionnaire in Appendix 3: knowledge and experience, financial situation, objectives, investment horizon, tolerance for losses. The questionnaire is used to inform the client, to advise them when they ask for it, and to detect transactions inconsistent with their profile.

By default, the client is categorised as a **non-professional** client. They may be categorised as a **professional** client only if they are an institutional investor or if they request it in writing and meet the criteria laid down by the regulations; they are then informed of the protection they are giving up.

### Article 11 — Step 7: risk rating

The desk rates the client's risk using the matrix in Appendix 1 to the AML/CFT Policy: **low**, **standard** or **high**. The rating proposed by the Guichet is a starting point, never a decision. The desk confirms or changes it, giving reasons for any change.

The following are rated at least **standard** risk: legal persons, groups, residents outside Cameroon and any remote onboarding. The following are rated **high** risk: PEPs and their close associates and family members, and clients presenting a high-risk factor in the matrix.

### Article 12 — Step 8: decision

| Risk | Who decides | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Desk | Complete file; review sheet (Appendix 2) signed |
| Standard | Desk; RCCI review within one month | Complete file; review sheet signed |
| High or PEP | Chief Executive Officer, after the RCCI's written opinion | Enhanced due diligence: evidence of the source of funds and, for a PEP, of the source of wealth; approval sheet in Appendix 5 to the AML/CFT Policy |

The desk decides within **five business days** of receipt of a complete file. It may request additional information; the file then remains on hold. If the client does not respond within three months, the file is closed with no further action.

A refusal is notified to the client without detailed reasons. The actual reason is recorded in the file. If the refusal is based on a suspicion, the desk informs the RCCI.

### Article 13 — Step 9: agreement and account opening

Once the file has been approved:

1. the client **accepts the securities account opening agreement** (PC-CTR-004), together with the schedule of fees and the risk disclosure notice: in the Guichet, by one-time code, with the time and channel recorded; at the branch, by signing two original copies;
2. the desk sends the file for opening the client's registered sub-account to the account keeper and, for government securities, to the primary dealer (spécialiste en valeurs du Trésor);
3. the desk enters the account number assigned and informs the client.

If the agreement changes, the client accepts the new version before any further transaction.

### Article 14 — Step 10: first payment

The first payment must come from the declared bank account, in the client's name. The desk checks that the name of the originator of the transfer matches the client's name. A payment from a third party is refused and returned to the sender. For a client onboarded remotely, the match is recorded in the file as a verification element.

## Title III — The ongoing relationship

### Article 15 — Periodic update

The file is updated at least every **two years** for low-risk or standard-risk clients, and **every year** for high-risk clients. The Guichet flags files due for review and documents that have expired.

The update repeats Steps 3 to 7 for anything that has changed, and always includes screening. If the client does not respond within three months, new transactions are blocked and the relationship is then terminated; the RCCI is informed.

### Article 16 — Event-driven update

The file is reviewed without waiting for the due date where:

- the client reports a change of address, country of residence, occupation, representative or beneficial owner;
- a transaction is inconsistent with their profile or the declared source of funds;
- a document expires;
- a sanctions list is amended and shows a possible match.

### Article 17 — Change of the declared bank account

Changing the bank account is the most common means of diverting a client's funds. It is subject to strict rules:

- the request is made by the client personally, in their client area or on the signed form in Appendix 4;
- the new RIB is in the client's name;
- the desk confirms the request by calling the telephone number already recorded in the file, never a number given in the request;
- no payment is made to the new account until these checks have been completed.

### Article 18 — Closure and transfer

At the client's request, or on the Company's initiative under the conditions of the agreement, the desk:

- transfers the securities to the intermediary designated by the client, or sells them on the client's instructions;
- pays the cash balance into the declared bank account;
- provides a final statement;
- changes the file status to "closed".

The file is kept for ten years after closure.

## Title IV — Retention and control

### Article 19 — Retention

Documents, review sheets, screening attestations, decisions and the agreement are kept for **ten years** from the end of the relationship, in the Guichet or, for paper originals, in the Company's locked cabinet. They are kept available to COSUMAF and ANIF.

### Article 20 — Controls

| Level | Who | What | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First level | Desk | Complete review sheet for each file; no account opened without approval | For each file |
| Second level | RCCI | Review of all standard-risk files; sample of low-risk files; all high-risk files | Every month |
| Second level | RCCI | Files due for update and overdue; average processing time; completeness of screening | Every quarter |

Findings are handled in accordance with the Internal Control and Compliance Charter.

### Article 21 — Entry into force

This procedure enters into force on the date of its approval, as indicated in the document control sheet. It is reviewed every year and whenever the AML/CFT Policy is amended.
