Documents must be valid; proof of address must be less than three months old. Documents marked ★ are required by the Guichet before the file can be submitted; the others are requested depending on the risk.

| | Natural person | Legal person | Group (tontine, association, joint ownership) | Institutional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Surname, first names, date and place of birth, nationality, occupation, address, telephone, email | Corporate name, legal form, registered office, registration, business activity | Name, legal form, address, purpose | Name, regulatory status, licence |
| Identity documents | ★ Identity document front and back; ★ selfie (onboarding in the Guichet) | ★ RCCM extract (trade register); ★ up-to-date articles of association; ★ identity document of the representative | ★ Receipt of declaration or constitutive instrument; ★ identity document of the representative | ★ RCCM extract or licence |
| Address and tax | ★ Proof of address; ★ NIU (taxpayer identification number) certificate (residents) | ★ NIU certificate | NIU certificate if one exists | NIU certificate |
| Powers | Power of attorney not accepted (agreement, Article 9); legal representative for a minor or a protected adult | ★ Powers or minutes appointing the signatories | ★ Minutes appointing the authorised agents and the decision-making rule; ★ list of members | ★ Signatory matrix and limits |
| Beneficial owners | The client themselves, unless there is evidence to the contrary | ★ Declaration of beneficial owners (more than 25%), with the date of birth of each | Members exercising control, with date of birth | According to the control structure |
| Bank account | ★ RIB in the client's name | ★ RIB in the company's name | ★ RIB in the group's name | ★ RIB in the institution's name |
| Funds and profile | ★ Source of funds, amounts envisaged; ★ questionnaire (Appendix 3) | ★ Source of funds; ★ questionnaire; latest financial statements if high risk | ★ Source of funds (contributions, collective savings); ★ questionnaire; ceiling of FCFA 25,000,000 for a joint ownership | ★ Source of funds; ★ questionnaire |
| Declarations | ★ PEP; ★ data consent | ★ PEP for representatives and beneficial owners; ★ data consent | ★ PEP for authorised agents; ★ data consent | ★ Data consent |
| Resident outside CEMAC | Video interview (Appendix 5); foreign proof of address; NIU not required | Equivalent documents, legalised or apostilled | Not accepted without the RCCI's opinion | Equivalent documents |
| High risk or PEP | Evidence of the source of funds (statements, deed of sale, employment contract) and, for a PEP, of the source of wealth | Same, plus open-source search on the senior executives | Same | Same |
