nature: "Undertaking"

In using the tools of Purpose Capital S.A., I undertake to comply with the following rules.

**My access**
1. I use only my named account, with multi-factor authentication. I never lend my access and I never disclose a password or code to anyone, not even to a senior manager or to the IT service provider.
2. I use a different password for each service, kept in the Company's password vault.

**My equipment**
3. I process client data only on equipment supplied by the Company. I lock my workstation whenever I leave it.
4. I do not install any software and I do not plug in any unknown USB stick.
5. I immediately report the loss or theft of my computer or telephone.

**Information**
6. I save my documents in the Company's storage spaces, never in a personal service or by forwarding them to my personal email.
7. I send client data only to people who need it, through the Company's channels. I neither receive nor transmit orders through a personal messaging service.
8. I do not discuss confidential information in a public place and I keep my screen out of sight of third parties.

**Fraud**
9. I do not act on any request received by email for a payment, a change of bank details or a password without having confirmed it through another channel, using the number already on file.
10. If I am in doubt about a message, I do not click and I report it to the IT officer.

**Incidents**
11. I immediately report any incident or doubt to the IT officer and the RCCI, without trying to resolve it alone.

**Monitoring**
12. I am aware that use of the Company's tools is logged, in compliance with the law, and that any breach may lead to disciplinary sanctions.
