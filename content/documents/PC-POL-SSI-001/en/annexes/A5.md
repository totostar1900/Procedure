The "In place" column is ticked at each check; a missing measure becomes an action in the control plan.

| Tool | Minimum measures | Who | Check | In place |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Laptops | Disk encryption; automatic updates; active antivirus; locking after 5 minutes; no administrative rights for the user; remote wipe possible | Service provider | Six-monthly | ☐ |
| Business telephones | Lock code; updates; remote wipe; business apps only for client data | IT officer | Six-monthly | ☐ |
| Online email and office applications | Multi-factor authentication for all; security defaults enabled; emergency account under seal; external automatic forwarding blocked; login alerts; SPF, DKIM and DMARC published | Service provider, Chief Executive Officer | Quarterly | ☐ |
| Guichet | Second factor mandatory on the desk; segregation of proposal and approval; chained audit log checked; review before merging into the production branch; secrets in protected variables; API spending caps | Guichet manager | Quarterly | ☐ |
| Guichet database | Named console access with second factor; automatic backups and external copy; service key never exposed client-side | Guichet manager | Quarterly | ☐ |
| Website and domain name | Second factor with the hosting provider and the registrar; domain transfer lock; automatic renewal; HTTPS certificate | Chief Executive Officer | Annual | ☐ |
| Online banking | Dual signature of payments; individual tokens; caps; alerts | Chief Executive Officer | Six-monthly | ☐ |
| Password vault | Second factor; sharing by folder on a need basis; emergency access defined | IT officer | Six-monthly | ☐ |
| Backups | Three copies, two media, one offline or with another provider; restoration tested | Service provider | Quarterly | ☐ |
| Office network | Strong Wi-Fi password; separate guest network; up-to-date equipment; dual internet access | Service provider | Annual | ☐ |
