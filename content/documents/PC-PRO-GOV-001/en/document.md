## Title I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

This procedure sets out how Purpose Capital S.A. (the "Company") creates, has checked, approves, distributes, updates and withdraws its reference documents: codes, policies, procedures, contract templates, operating instructions and forms.

Its aim is simple: **everyone must work with the right version of a document, know who approved it, and be able to prove it to COSUMAF.**

### Article 2 — Scope

The procedure applies to all documents in the repository and to their appendices. It does not apply to documents produced for a client or a transaction (signed agreements, notices, statements), which follow the Archiving and Retention Procedure.

### Article 3 — Definitions

- **Repository**: all of the Company's reference documents, which can be consulted on the repository website.
- **Source**: the text file from which a document is produced. It is the only version that is edited.
- **Version in force**: the latest approved version of a document, published on the repository website.
- **Uncontrolled copy**: any printed or downloaded copy of a document. It may be out of date as early as the next day.
- **Owner**: the function responsible for the content of a document and for keeping it up to date.
- **Transaction file**: all the data entered to complete a contract template for a given client or transaction.

## Title II — Organisation of the repository

### Article 4 — Document levels and approval workflow

| Level | Prefix | Drafts | Reviews | Approves | Review cycle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Code | COD | RCCI | Chief Executive Officer | Board of Directors | Annual |
| Policy | POL | Owner of the area | RCCI | Board of Directors | Annual |
| Procedure | PRO | Process owner | RCCI | Chief Executive Officer | Every 1 to 2 years |
| Contract template | CTR | Head of the department concerned | RCCI and legal counsel | Chief Executive Officer | Annual |
| Operating instructions | MOP | Head of the unit | RCCI | Head of the unit | As needed |
| Form | FRM | Owner of the parent document | RCCI | Approver of the parent document | With the parent document |

A lower-level document may not contradict a higher-level document. In the event of a contradiction, the higher level applies and the lower-level document is corrected.

The person who drafts a document may not be the only person to review it. Where staffing does not allow this, the review is carried out by another function or, failing that, this is noted in the register.

### Article 5 — Referencing

Each document is given a unique and permanent reference:

- **PC-COD-NNN** for a code, **PC-CTR-NNN** for a contract template;
- **PC-POL-XXX-NNN**, **PC-PRO-XXX-NNN** or **PC-MOP-XXX-NNN** for a policy, a procedure or operating instructions, XXX being a three-letter code for the subject (LBC, KYC, ORD…);
- **the document reference followed by -A1, -A2…** for its appendices.

A reference is never reused, even after the document has been withdrawn.

### Article 6 — Versions and statuses

| Number | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0.1 to 0.9 | Draft, being written or reviewed |
| 1.0 | First approved version |
| 1.1, 1.2… | Minor change: correction, clarification, update of a contact or a table, with no change of rule |
| 2.0, 3.0… | Major change: new rule, new role, new threshold, new obligation |

A document has one of the following statuses: **To be drafted** (planned in the register), **Draft**, **In force**, **Repealed**. As long as a document is not in force, its PDFs carry a "Draft" watermark.

Any change to the text, however minor, must be signed off again by the usual reviewer and approver: the sign-off relates to the fingerprint of the text, which changes with the slightest modification. The difference concerns reading:

- **minor correction** (1.1, 1.2…): acknowledgements of the previous version remain valid; nobody has to read it again;
- **revision** (2.0, 3.0…): all designated persons are asked to read it again, as soon as it is adopted.

The RCCI classifies the change when opening it; where there is doubt, it is a revision.

**Draft revision.** While a revision is being prepared and signed off, the version in force remains online and continues to be authoritative. The draft is published alongside it, clearly marked as such, with its own PDFs and its own sign-offs. It replaces the version in force only upon adoption; the replaced version is then archived.

### Article 7 — Control sheet

Each document begins with a control sheet showing: its reference, its version and status, its owner, the approval workflow with dates, the version history, its distribution, its next review date and the reference texts on which it is based, with a link where the text is published online.

## Title III — Life cycle of a document

### Article 8 — Request

Anyone may request the creation or modification of a document using Appendix 1, or by a simple message to the RCCI. A modification is mandatory where:

- a CEMAC, COSUMAF, BVMAC or ANIF text changes;
- a control, an incident, a complaint or a recommendation shows that a rule is inappropriate;
- the organisation, a tool or a service provider changes;
- the periodic review so provides.

The RCCI records the request in the register (Appendix 3) and designates the author.

### Article 9 — Drafting

The author starts from the latest version in force. The author writes in plain language that everyone can understand: short sentences, one subject per article, tables for responsibilities and deadlines. People are referred to by their function, never by name.

The author notes in the control sheet any points still to be confirmed and any inconsistencies found with other documents or with the Guichet.

### Article 10 — Review

Using the review sheet (Appendix 2), the reviewer checks that the document:

- complies with the regulations and the reference texts cited;
- is consistent with the other documents in the repository and with the Guichet;
- can be applied by the Company's actual staff;
- provides for the controls and the evidence to be kept.

### Article 11 — Approval

The approver approves the document, has it corrected, or rejects it. Approval by the Board of Directors is recorded in the minutes of the meeting; approval by the Chief Executive Officer by their sign-off.

**Electronic sign-offs.** Review, approval and adoption are given on the repository website, in the "Sign-offs and approvals" box of each document:

- each person signs off personally, from their own account; nobody signs off on behalf of anyone else;
- a sign-off is accepted only from the person to whom the repository administrator (the Chairman of the Board of Directors) has assigned the required function, and in order: review, then approval;
- adoption by the Board is recorded by the Chairman, with the date of the meeting and the reference of the minutes, which remain the evidence of the decision;
- each sign-off bears the date, the time and the **fingerprint** of the text signed off; if the text subsequently changes, the sign-off is no longer valid and must be given again;
- the register of sign-offs is exported at least once a quarter by the RCCI and kept with the repository archives.

For the first six months, the electronic sign-off is backed up by the signed review sheet (Appendix 2). The approval date and the date of entry into force are then entered in the control sheet; the version becomes 1.0 or the next number and the status becomes "In force".

### Article 12 — Publication and distribution

Within two business days of approval, the RCCI:

1. publishes the new version on the repository website, with its PDFs and appendices;
2. informs the people on the distribution list, stating in a few lines what has changed;
3. collects, within one month, for the codes, policies and procedures that concern them, the Staff Members' acknowledgement: by means of the "I have read and understood" button on the website for the persons designated by the administrator, or otherwise by means of Appendix 4;
4. organises a short presentation where the change alters the way people work.

### Article 13 — Periodic review

Each document is reviewed no later than the next review date shown on its control sheet. The review may conclude that no change is needed: the review date is then updated and the version does not change. Every year, the RCCI presents the status of the repository to the Board of Directors: documents in force, in draft, to be drafted, and overdue reviews.

### Article 14 — Withdrawal

A document is repealed when it no longer serves any purpose or when it is replaced. Withdrawal follows the document's approval workflow. The status becomes "Repealed" and the documents that referred to it are corrected.

### Article 15 — Retention of versions

All approved versions, including repealed ones, are kept with their history for **ten years** after they are replaced. They make it possible to know which rule applied on a given date, which COSUMAF may ask during an inspection.

## Title IV — The repository in practice

### Article 16 — One source, several formats

Each document is written once only, in a source file. The repository website, the PDF of the document, the PDFs of each appendix and the index are produced automatically from that source. A PDF or a page of the website is never edited by hand: the source is edited and then everything is regenerated.

The sources are kept in a space to which access is restricted to the RCCI and to the persons the RCCI designates, and are backed up in accordance with the Business Continuity Plan.

### Article 17 — Printed copies

Only the version published on the repository website is authoritative. Each printed page bears the statement "Uncontrolled printed copy — only the online version is authoritative". Before using a printed copy or a downloaded PDF, everyone checks on the website that the version is still in force.

### Article 18 — Contract templates and transaction files

A contract template is never completed by hand. It is completed using the repository form: each item of data is entered only once, the variants are selected, and the contract in Word or PDF is produced only when all mandatory fields are completed and consistent.

Each transaction file keeps the version of the template used. If the template changes, files in progress are reviewed before signature.

### Article 19 — Alignment with the Guichet

Some texts exist both in the repository and in the Guichet, in particular the securities account opening agreement, the schedule of fees and the risk disclosure notice. For these texts:

- the repository text and the Guichet text must be identical;
- any change is made **on the same day** in both, by the RCCI or under the RCCI's control;
- the register (Appendix 3) shows the correspondence between the repository version and the Guichet version;
- a change affecting clients is notified to them before it enters into force, under the conditions laid down in the agreement.

## Title V — Control

### Article 20 — Register and control

The RCCI maintains the register of documents (Appendix 3). Every quarter, the RCCI checks:

- that no reviews are overdue;
- that the versions published on the website correspond to the approved versions;
- that the texts shared with the Guichet are identical;
- that acknowledgements have been collected.

Discrepancies are dealt with in accordance with the Internal Control and Compliance Charter.

### Article 21 — Entry into force

This procedure enters into force on the date of its approval, as indicated in the document control sheet.
