## Title I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

This procedure sets out how Purpose Capital S.A. (the "Company") prepares, has approved, publishes, records and withdraws its marketing communications. It implements Article 42 of the Code of Conduct and Article 20 of the Internal Control and Compliance Charter (prior opinion of the RCCI).

The aim is simple: a client or prospect who reads any content from the Company should come away with an accurate picture, neither rosier nor more alarming than reality.

### Article 2 — What counts as a marketing communication

A marketing communication is any content intended for clients or the public that presents the Company, its services or a product, whatever the medium:

- the purposecapital.africa website, the brochure and leaflets;
- posts on LinkedIn and other social media;
- press releases, interviews and articles;
- content published in the Guichet: offer sheets, plain-language notes on auctions, yield simulators, "CEMAC Opportunity" pages;
- messages sent to several people by WhatsApp, SMS or email;
- presentations given to prospects;
- any advertising of a public offering.

The following are not covered: individual exchanges with a client about their own account, contract notes and statements, and the information memorandum submitted for COSUMAF approval (visa), which follows its own workflow with the issuer.

### Article 3 — Who does what

| Function | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Author (any Staff Member) | Prepares the content, completes the approval request (Appendix 1), attaches the sources of the figures |
| Head of Sales | Owner of the procedure; checks commercial accuracy; maintains the register (Appendix 3) |
| RCCI | Gives a prior written opinion, using the checklist (Appendix 2); carries out ex post control |
| Chief Executive Officer | Approves major materials (Article 8); the only person, together with the Chairman of the Board of Directors, entitled to speak on behalf of the Company |

## Title II — Substantive rules

### Article 4 — Clear, accurate, balanced, not misleading

Every communication:

1. **is accurate**: every figure has a source and a date, and can be verified;
2. **is clear**: it can be understood without any particular financial knowledge; technical terms are explained;
3. **is balanced**: when it mentions a benefit (yield, security, liquidity), it mentions the corresponding risks with equal prominence;
4. **leaves out nothing important**: fees, investment horizon, risk of capital loss, taxation;
5. **is identifiable**: promotional content is presented as such, never disguised as neutral information or as a press article.

### Article 5 — What is prohibited

The following are prohibited:

- promising, or giving the appearance of, a guaranteed return, unless there is a genuine legal guarantee that is described;
- the words "risk-free", "safe" or "certain" applied to an investment;
- comparison with another product or a competitor that is not based on comparable, sourced and dated information;
- client testimonials presented as representative, or not genuinely collected with the client's written consent;
- highlighting past performance without a warning;
- use of the name, logo or licence of COSUMAF, BVMAC or BEAC to suggest that they recommend a product;
- any inside or confidential information about a client or an issuer.

### Article 6 — Mandatory statements

Depending on the medium, the communication carries the statements in Appendix 4, in particular:

- the identity of the Company and its COSUMAF licence No. COSUMAF-SDB-01/2026;
- for a yield: the words "before tax" and the calculation method (yield to maturity, coupon, at maturity);
- for past performance: "past performance is not a reliable indicator of future performance", the period and the source;
- for a simulation: "simulation for illustrative purposes only", and the assumptions used;
- for a product: a reminder of the risk of capital loss where it exists, and of liquidity risk;
- for a public offering: the number and date of the COSUMAF approval (visa), and where the information memorandum can be consulted free of charge. All advertising is consistent with that document, which prevails.

### Article 7 — Personal data and direct marketing

- A promotional message by WhatsApp, SMS or email is sent only to people who have consented to it; the Guichet records that consent.
- Every message states how to stop receiving such messages (keyword STOP for WhatsApp, link or reply for email); a request to stop is acted upon without delay, in accordance with the account agreement (Article 7) and Cameroonian Law No. 2024/017.
- No contact list bought or obtained from third parties is used.
- Campaigns target only countries where the Company is authorised to address the public; a campaign aimed at the diaspora or outside CEMAC is subject to the prior opinion of the RCCI.

## Title III — The approval workflow

### Article 8 — Approval levels

| Level | Content | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine | LinkedIn post with no figures or products; reuse of content already approved; Company news | Opinion of the RCCI (may be given by written message) |
| Standard | Offer sheet, note on an auction, message to several clients, presentation to a prospect, change to a website page | Written opinion of the RCCI on the checklist (Appendix 2) |
| Major | Brochure, new website page or redesign, press release, campaign, advertising of a public offering, simulator | Written opinion of the RCCI, then approval by the Chief Executive Officer |

Where there is doubt about the level, the higher level applies.

### Article 9 — Process

1. **Drafting**: the author prepares the content and completes the request (Appendix 1), with the source of each figure.
2. **Opinion of the RCCI**: within two business days, or the same day for a note linked to an imminent auction. The opinion is favourable, favourable subject to corrections, or unfavourable, and always gives reasons.
3. **Approval** by the Chief Executive Officer for major content.
4. **Publication** of the content exactly as approved; any change, however minor, to a figure or statement goes back to the RCCI.
5. **Recording** in the register (Appendix 3), with a copy of the published content.

The RCCI never approves content that the RCCI has drafted: such content is then approved by the Chief Executive Officer.

### Article 10 — Content published in the Guichet

Texts published in the Guichet are held in its register of "passages", which assigns each a sensitivity level:

- **free**: service texts with no commercial purpose (navigation, help);
- **reviewed**: routine commercial content, in force after review by a second member of the desk;
- **regulatory**: risk warnings, legal notices, terms and conditions and any text that commits the Company, in force after approval by a manager.

Commercial content is never classified as "free". Every quarter, the RCCI checks the classification of passages and the versions in force.

### Article 11 — Urgency

Where content must be published without delay (incident, correction, market information), the Chief Executive Officer may authorise publication; the opinion of the RCCI is obtained no later than the next business day and the content is corrected if necessary.

## Title IV — After publication

### Article 12 — Register and retention

The Head of Sales maintains the register of communications (Appendix 3). Each published item, with its approval request, the opinion of the RCCI and, where applicable, the approval of the Chief Executive Officer, is kept for ten years, in accordance with the Archiving and Retention Procedure.

### Article 13 — Updating and withdrawal

Content is updated or withdrawn as soon as it becomes inaccurate: rates or terms changed, offer closed, regulatory change, change of senior manager. Each item has a review date in the register; the permanent pages of the website and the brochure are reviewed at least once a year. Withdrawal is noted in the register.

### Article 14 — Personal social media

Staff Members may share the Company's official posts without altering them. In a personal capacity, they do not publish any recommendation on a security or any information about a client, an issuer or an ongoing transaction, and do not speak on behalf of the Company (Code of Conduct, Article 42).

### Article 15 — Ex post control

Every quarter, the RCCI checks a sample of published content: presence in the register, consistency with the approved content, figures up to date, consents for bulk mailings. Complaints that concern a communication are analysed. Findings are handled in accordance with the Internal Control and Compliance Charter.

### Article 16 — Entry into force

This procedure enters into force on the date of its approval, as indicated in the document control sheet. It is reviewed every year.
