## Title I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

This policy sets out how Purpose Capital S.A. (the "Company") ensures that every person acting on its behalf is **of good repute**, **competent** and **trained** in the rules they must apply.

It sets:

- the checks carried out before hiring or appointment, and then every year;
- the mandatory training, for whom and how often;
- the minimum number of continuing professional development hours;
- how the Company checks that the rules are known;
- the evidence kept and the reporting to the Board of Directors.

### Article 2 — Why this policy matters

A brokerage firm (société de bourse) works on trust: its clients entrust it with their securities and funds, and COSUMAF entrusts it with a licence. A dishonest or poorly trained person can, in a single transaction, cause a loss to a client, a fraud, undetected money laundering or a breach that is sanctioned.

The Company is small: everyone holds several roles and there is not always a second Staff Member to catch an error. The competence and integrity of each person are therefore the first line of defence.

### Article 3 — Scope

The policy applies:

- to salaried **Staff Members**, whatever their contract;
- to **senior managers (dirigeants effectifs)**: the Chief Executive Officer and, if one is appointed, the Deputy Chief Executive Officer;
- to **directors**, including the Chairman of the Board of Directors;
- to **interns** and temporary staff;
- to **service providers and agents** who act in the name of the Company or have access to its systems, premises or client information (IT service provider, external accountant, business introducer), under the conditions of Article 7.

### Article 4 — Definitions and cross-references

- **Good repute**: the quality of a person whose integrity, honesty and reputation leave no doubt that they will perform their duties fairly and in compliance with the law.
- **Competence**: the knowledge, qualifications, experience and authorisations needed to hold a given position.
- **Card holder**: a person performing a function for which COSUMAF requires a professional card or an authorisation.
- **Online repository**: the site where the Company's documents are published, where each person acknowledges having read them and takes the knowledge tests. Its records constitute evidence.
- **Continuing professional development**: any activity that maintains or develops skills useful for the position, in the formats set out in Article 19.

This policy relies on the following documents, without repeating them: the Code of Conduct (PC-COD-001), the AML/CFT Policy (PC-POL-LBC-001), the Personal Transactions Procedure (PC-PRO-OPP-001), the Information Systems Security Policy (PC-POL-SSI-001), the Internal Control and Compliance Charter (PC-COD-003) and the Remuneration Policy (PC-POL-REM-001).

## Title II — Organisation

### Article 5 — Roles

| Function | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Board of Directors | Approves the policy and the annual training budget; checks the good repute and competence of the Chief Executive Officer and of proposed directors; attends its own training every year; reviews the annual report |
| Chairman of the Board of Directors | Conducts the checks concerning the Chief Executive Officer and the directors, with the support of the RCCI; receives declarations of change in circumstances from the Chief Executive Officer and the directors |
| Chief Executive Officer | Owner of the policy; decides on hiring after the checks; approves the annual training plan; makes time and budget available; checks the good repute and training of the RCCI; decides on measures when a Staff Member's circumstances change |
| RCCI | Conducts pre-hiring checks; monitors professional cards; prepares the annual plan; runs internal training; writes the tests; runs the unannounced tests; keeps the training register; reports |
| Line managers | Identify their team's needs; support induction; ensure that training is completed on time; review training at the annual appraisal |
| Each Staff Member | Tells the truth during checks; declares any change in circumstances without delay; completes their training and acknowledges their reading on time; completes their continuing professional development hours and provides evidence |

The RCCI does not check their own files: their checks, card and training are monitored by the Chief Executive Officer. The Chief Executive Officer's files are monitored by the Chairman of the Board.

## Title III — Good repute and competence

### Article 6 — Requirements

**Good repute.** No person may work for the Company, or hold office in it, who:

- has been finally convicted of a serious crime, or of an offence of theft, fraud, breach of trust, corruption, forgery, money laundering, terrorist financing, a stock market, tax or customs offence, or any offence involving dishonesty;
- is subject to personal bankruptcy, a ban on managing a company or a ban on practising a financial profession;
- has been sanctioned by COSUMAF, COBAC or another financial authority for a lack of integrity, or has been removed from a position on those grounds;
- appears on a targeted financial sanctions list;
- has knowingly provided false or incomplete information during the checks.

Any other situation (pending proceedings, major litigation, lasting conflict of interest, serious financial difficulties) is not an automatic bar: it is assessed case by case under Article 11, taking into account its seriousness, how long ago it occurred and the position concerned.

**Competence.** Each position has a job description stating the required qualification or experience and, where applicable, the professional card. A person who does not yet meet a condition may be recruited if a written training plan, with a deadline, enables them to meet it; they do not perform the function concerned alone until the condition is met.

### Article 7 — Checks before hiring

Checks are carried out **before the contract is signed**, or at the latest before the person starts work where the offer is conditional. The RCCI records them on the checklist in Appendix 5; the Chief Executive Officer does not sign the contract until the checklist is complete.

| Check | Staff Members | Senior managers, card holders, directors | Interns | Service providers acting for the Company |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identity document | Yes | Yes | Yes | Persons with access |
| Criminal record extract (bulletin No. 3) less than three months old, and foreign equivalent if the person has lived outside Cameroon for more than one year in the last five years | Yes | Yes, renewed every three years | Yes | Certificate from the provider that it has checked its personnel |
| Verification of the highest qualification required for the position, with the institution or on the original | Yes | Yes | Certificate of enrolment | No |
| Two references, at least one from a former employer, taken by telephone and recorded | Yes | Yes | One, from the institution | Company references |
| Declaration of interests (Code of Conduct, Appendix 3) | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Screening against sanctions and PEP lists (PC-POL-LBC-001, Article 21) and against sanctions published by COSUMAF and COBAC | Yes | Yes | Yes | Company and its managers |
| Search for adverse public information (press, internet) | Yes | Yes | No | Company |
| Declaration of good repute and competence (Appendix 4) | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |

Adverse information is never set aside without being recorded. The RCCI gives a written opinion to the Chief Executive Officer, who decides; for a director or the Chief Executive Officer, the opinion is given to the Chairman of the Board.

The contract with a service provider acting for the Company includes a clause by which it guarantees the good repute and competence of its personnel, undertakes to replace without delay any person who no longer meets these conditions, and has each of them sign the acknowledgement of adherence to the Code of Conduct and the acceptable use charter for digital tools (PC-POL-SSI-001, Appendix 1).

### Article 8 — Appointment of a senior manager or card holder

Before a senior manager is appointed, the RCCI prepares the file that the Company submits to COSUMAF for prior authorisation (General Regulation, Art. 76). The person takes up their duties only after that authorisation.

Before a person performs a function subject to a professional card, the Company files the application for the card or authorisation with COSUMAF (General Regulation, Art. 24 and 25). Until the card is issued, the person does not carry out the reserved acts alone: a card holder of the Company carries them out or approves them.

Before proposing a director to the general meeting or to the Board, the Chairman of the Board ensures, with the RCCI, that the checks in Article 7 have been made and that the candidate adds to the collective skills of the Board (finance and markets, law, control and risk, accounting, technology).

The same checks are repeated when a Staff Member moves to a senior manager or card holder function.

### Article 9 — Professional cards

The RCCI keeps the list of cards in the training register (Appendix 3): holder, function, number, date of issue, expiry date, renewal conditions.

- **Three months before expiry**, the RCCI notifies the holder and the Chief Executive Officer.
- The renewal file is filed **no later than two months before expiry**, or within any longer period set by COSUMAF.
- If a card expires or is suspended, its holder immediately stops carrying out the reserved acts; the Chief Executive Officer arranges cover and the RCCI informs COSUMAF under the conditions laid down by the regulations.

The expiry date of each card is entered in the compliance calendar of the Internal Control Charter (PC-COD-003, Appendix 5).

### Article 10 — Annual declaration

Every year **before 31 January**, each Staff Member, senior manager and director completes the declaration of good repute and competence in Appendix 4, together with the annual declaration of interests under the Code of Conduct and the annual declaration of personal transactions (PC-PRO-OPP-001, Article 16). They confirm that their circumstances have not changed or describe what has changed.

The RCCI checks that all declarations have been received before 15 February and, at that time, re-screens all Staff Members and directors against the sanctions lists. Staff Members and directors are also included in the file screened at each list update, like clients (PC-POL-LBC-001, Article 21).

### Article 11 — Change in circumstances

Any person covered by the policy declares **without delay, and no later than five working days**, any fact that could call into question their good repute or competence, in particular:

- police custody, formal investigation, prosecution or criminal conviction;
- proceedings or a sanction by a financial authority or professional body;
- personal bankruptcy, insolvency proceedings or a significant seizure;
- the suspension, withdrawal or non-renewal of their professional card;
- a new office, outside activity or interest creating a lasting conflict.

The declaration is made to the RCCI; if the RCCI is concerned, to the Chief Executive Officer; if it concerns the Chief Executive Officer or a director, to the Chairman of the Board of Directors; if it concerns the Chairman, to the Board.

The RCCI, or the person receiving the declaration, assesses the situation within five working days and proposes measures: no measure, temporary removal of certain tasks, restricted access, four-eyes approval, change of position. The Chief Executive Officer decides; the Board decides for the Chief Executive Officer and the directors. These measures protect the Company and its clients; they do not prejudge the person's guilt. The RCCI considers whether COSUMAF should be informed, in particular for a senior manager or a card holder.

Failure to declare such a fact is misconduct within the meaning of Article 49 of the Code of Conduct.

### Article 12 — Protection of data from checks

Checks involve sensitive data. They are processed in compliance with Law No. 2024/017 on personal data protection:

- the candidate is informed in writing of the checks carried out and their purpose, and authorises them by signing Appendix 4;
- only information relevant to the position is collected;
- the documents are filed in the individual file, with access restricted to the Chief Executive Officer and the RCCI, under the Human Resources heading of the filing plan (PC-PRO-ARC-001);
- the documents of an unsuccessful candidate are destroyed within three months, unless there is a dispute.

## Title IV — Mandatory training

### Article 13 — Induction programme

Every person joining the Company follows the induction programme in Appendix 1. They read each document in the online repository, acknowledge having read it and take the knowledge test in Article 21.

| Module | Reference document | Maximum period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Code of Conduct | PC-COD-001 | 15 working days |
| AML/CFT and financial sanctions | PC-POL-LBC-001 | Before handling a client file or a payment, and no later than 15 working days |
| Conflicts of interest | PC-POL-CI-001 | 15 working days |
| Market abuse and personal transactions | PC-COD-001 (Title V) and PC-PRO-OPP-001 | Before any personal transaction, and no later than 15 working days |
| Confidentiality and IT security | PC-POL-SSI-001 | Before access is granted |
| Complaint handling | PC-PRO-REC-001 | 15 working days |
| Position-specific documents | Article 15 | Before performing the task concerned alone |

The programme also includes a one-hour meeting with the RCCI during the first week, to explain the rules, answer questions and present the whistleblowing arrangements. It is **completed no later than one month after arrival**, the period set by the Code of Conduct (Article 46).

Interns follow the Code, AML/CFT, and confidentiality and IT security modules; they do not handle any client file or order without supervision. A service provider's personnel follow the confidentiality and IT security module and sign the Code before any access.

The RCCI ticks off Appendix 1 and places it in the individual file. Until the programme is completed, the online repository reports delays to the RCCI and the line manager.

### Article 14 — Annual AML/CFT training

CEMAC regulations require continuing AML/CFT training for all senior managers and employees (Regulation No. 02/24). Every year, **before 30 November**, the AML/CFT officer organises training of at least **two hours** for all Staff Members, senior managers and directors. It covers:

- changes in the texts, COSUMAF guidelines and FATF lists;
- red flags specific to securities transactions (PC-POL-LBC-001, Appendix 3) and case studies;
- asset freezing and screening;
- findings from the year's controls and mistakes not to repeat.

Persons in contact with clients and the operations function receive an additional hour of case studies on client onboarding and payments. The training ends with a test (Article 21). An absent person catches up within the month, in an individual session or online.

### Article 15 — Position-specific training

| Position | Training | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relationship managers, sales function | Client onboarding and know your customer (PC-PRO-KYC-001), suitability, marketing communications (PC-PRO-COM-001) | On arrival, then at each revision of the documents |
| Operations function, desk | Reception and transmission of orders (PC-PRO-ORD-001), best execution (PC-POL-EXE-001), segregation and reconciliation (PC-PRO-SEG-001), BVMAC and central securities depository rules | On arrival, then at each change in market rules |
| Capital markets | COSUMAF issuance and approval rules, information memorandum, placement, allocation, inside information | On arrival, then one external session per year |
| Accounting (internal or external) | Chart of accounts and regulatory statements of brokerage firms, reporting to COSUMAF (PC-PRO-REP-001) | On arrival, then at each change in the statements |
| RCCI | Regulatory monitoring of COSUMAF, BVMAC, ANIF; control methods; training required for the renewal of their card | At least one external session per year |
| IT focal point, IT service provider | Measures under the Security Policy, incident management | On arrival, then every year |

A person performs a task **alone** only after completing the corresponding training; until then, their acts are approved by a trained person.

### Article 16 — Directors' training

Each new director receives, **within three months** of appointment, a two-hour induction session run by the Chief Executive Officer and the RCCI: the Company's activities and risks, the COSUMAF and BVMAC framework, the role and responsibilities of the Board, AML/CFT responsibilities, conflicts of interest, inside information and personal transactions. They acknowledge having read the Code of Conduct and the AML/CFT Policy.

Every year, the Board devotes at least **one two-hour training session**, preferably alongside a meeting, to a topic chosen with the Chairman: the annual AML/CFT training in Article 14, a regulatory development, governance or a major risk. Attendance is recorded in the register.

### Article 17 — New or revised documents

Whenever a new or revised document is published in the online repository, the designated persons acknowledge having read it **within ten working days**. For a major revision, the RCCI adds a knowledge test or a short presentation.

## Title V — Continuing professional development

### Article 18 — Minimum hours

Each person completes at least, per calendar year:

| Population | Hours per year |
| --- | --- |
| Senior managers | 20 |
| Professional card holders | 20, or more if COSUMAF requires it for the renewal of the card |
| Staff Members in contact with clients or operations | 15 |
| Support functions | 10 |
| Directors | 6 |

The mandatory training in Articles 14 to 17 counts towards these hours. A person joining during the year completes a number of hours in proportion to their time with the Company. A shortfall of more than 25% at 30 September is reported to the line manager, who agrees with the person a catch-up plan before 31 December.

### Article 19 — Recognised formats

| Format | How hours are counted | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| External training (training body, university, professional association) | Actual duration | Certificate of attendance or completion |
| Session organised by COSUMAF, BVMAC, the central securities depository, BEAC or ANIF | Actual duration | Invitation and certificate, or attendance list |
| E-learning | Duration stated by the provider, capped at half of the annual hours | Certificate of completion |
| Internal session run by the RCCI or a Staff Member | Actual duration | Attendance list and materials |
| Preparation for a recognised professional certification | Duration of courses, capped at half of the annual hours | Enrolment and certificate |

Simply reading documents does not count, unless followed by a passed test (half an hour per test). Each person gives their evidence to the RCCI within one month of the training.

### Article 20 — Budget

The annual training budget is included in the budget approved by the Board of Directors, on a proposal from the Chief Executive Officer. It covers at least the mandatory training and the training needed to renew cards. Training normally takes place during working time.

External training costing more than FCFA 1,000,000 is authorised in writing by the Chief Executive Officer. It may be subject to a written undertaking by the person to remain in the Company's service for a reasonable period, or to repay part of the cost in the event of voluntary departure, within the limits allowed by the Labour Code.

## Title VI — Verification of knowledge

### Article 21 — Acknowledgement tests

When a person acknowledges having read a document in the induction programme, a position-specific document or a revised document, they take a test in the online repository:

- **five questions** drawn at random from a bank of at least ten questions per document, written by the RCCI and reviewed at each revision of the document;
- pass mark of **80%**, i.e. four correct answers out of five;
- **two attempts**; the second takes place no earlier than the following day;
- the correct answer and its explanation are shown at the end of the test.

The online repository keeps the date, the document, the version, the questions asked and the result. These records are the evidence of training.

### Article 22 — Unannounced tests

The RCCI runs, **at least twice a year** and without notice, a five-question test for all Staff Members, on topics chosen according to risks, incidents and control findings. Senior managers take part like everyone else. The test is taken in the online repository or, failing that, on a form entered in the register.

Unannounced tests are used to check that the rules are known in day-to-day work, not to catch people out: the overall results are presented to the team with the correct answers.

### Article 23 — Follow-up of failures

| Situation | Follow-up | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Failure at both attempts of a test | Re-reading of the document; meeting of at least thirty minutes with the RCCI or the line manager; new test | Ten working days |
| Further failure | Chief Executive Officer informed; written individual plan; the person's acts in the area concerned are approved by a trained person until they pass | Plan within five working days |
| Failure in an unannounced test | Meeting with the RCCI on the points missed | Ten working days |
| Topic missed by several people | Group session; the RCCI considers whether the document needs clarifying | Before the next unannounced test |

A failure is not misconduct. However, failing to attend mandatory training, failing to acknowledge reading after a reminder or refusing a test is a breach within the meaning of Article 49 of the Code of Conduct. Completion of training is one of the compliance criteria in the annual appraisal (PC-POL-REM-001, Article 17).

## Title VII — Planning, evidence and reporting

### Article 24 — Annual training plan

Before 15 December, the RCCI prepares with the line managers the training plan for the following year using the template in Appendix 2. It is based on the needs expressed at annual appraisals, mandatory training, card expiry dates, control findings, incidents, test results and regulatory developments.

The Chief Executive Officer approves the plan within the budget. The plan is presented to the Board with the budget. It is reviewed at mid-year.

### Article 25 — Register and individual files

The RCCI keeps the **training register** in Appendix 3: training completed, hours, test results, professional cards, annual declarations. It is one of the compliance registers of the Internal Control Charter (PC-COD-003, Article 22).

Each person has an **individual file** containing the documents from the checks, the job description, the completed Appendix 1, the annual declarations, the training certificates and, where applicable, their professional card. Each person may consult their own file on request.

### Article 26 — Reporting

| Recipient | Frequency | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Risk and compliance committee | Every month | Overdue induction programmes; unacknowledged readings; cards expiring within three months; failures being followed up |
| Board of Directors | Every year, in the annual internal control report (PC-COD-003, Appendix 4) | Plan completion rate; hours by population; AML/CFT training; test results; status of cards; annual declarations received; changes in circumstances and measures taken; plan for the following year |
| COSUMAF | With the annual internal control report, and on request | Same items |

The annual AML/CFT report includes the AML/CFT training data (PC-POL-LBC-001, Article 28).

### Article 27 — Retention

The register, individual files, test results and evidence are kept for **ten years from the person's departure** (PC-PRO-ARC-001), subject to Article 12 for unsuccessful candidates.

## Title VIII — Final provisions

### Article 28 — Control

Each year the RCCI includes in their control plan a check of the application of the policy. As the RCCI is its main actor, the annual independent review provided for by the Internal Control Charter also examines a sample of individual files and the register.

### Article 29 — Review and entry into force

The policy is reviewed every year by the RCCI with the Chief Executive Officer, and at each change in the regulations on professional cards, good repute or training. Any amendment is approved by the Board of Directors.

This policy enters into force on the date of its adoption by the Board of Directors, as shown in the document control sheet.
