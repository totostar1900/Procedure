**Rating.** Each risk is given a probability score and an impact score from 1 to 4. The risk level is the product of the two: **1 to 3 low · 4 to 6 moderate · 8 to 9 high · 12 to 16 critical**.

| Score | Probability | Financial impact | Client, regulatory or reputational impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rare: less than once in five years | Less than FCFA 1,000,000 | No client affected; no breach |
| 2 | Possible: once in two to five years | FCFA 1 million to 10 million | A few clients affected, with no loss; minor breach corrected |
| 3 | Likely: once a year | FCFA 10 million to 30 million | Loss for clients; COSUMAF observation; press article |
| 4 | Frequent: several times a year | More than FCFA 30 million or own funds threshold reached | COSUMAF sanction; licence called into question; loss of partners |
