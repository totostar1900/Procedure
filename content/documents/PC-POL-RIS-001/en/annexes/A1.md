**Risk appetite statement.** Purpose Capital S.A. accepts the risks inherent in its business as an intermediary — operational risk, business risk and reputational risk — within the limits below. It refuses any risk to its clients' assets, any proprietary speculation and any regulatory breach.

| Area | Appetite | Indicator | Alert threshold (amber) | Limit (red) | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Own funds | Low | Own funds | FCFA 300,000,000 + 3 months of fixed costs | FCFA 300,000,000 | Chief Executive Officer |
| Liquidity | Low | Available own cash, in months of fixed costs | 9 months | 6 months | Chief Executive Officer |
| Bank concentration | Low | Share of own cash held with the largest bank | 50% | 60% | Chief Executive Officer |
| Proprietary positions | None, other than cash management | Securities held for own account other than cash investments | — | FCFA 0 | Chief Executive Officer |
| Firm underwriting | Low | Firm underwriting or guarantee commitments, as % of own funds | 15% | 25% without Board approval | Head of Capital Markets |
| Client assets | None | Reconciliation differences unexplained after 2 business days | — | 0 | Chief Executive Officer |
| Credit to clients | None | Orders transmitted without cover | — | 0 | Chief Executive Officer |
| Execution errors | Low | Number of errors per quarter | 2 | 4 or any error > FCFA 1,000,000 | Chief Executive Officer |
| Non-compliance | None | Regulatory breaches identified; late submissions to COSUMAF | — | 0 | RCCI |
| AML/CFT | None | Client files incomplete beyond 3 months | 2% of files | 5% of files | RCCI |
| Revenue concentration | Moderate | Share of revenues from the largest client over a rolling 12 months | 30% | 50% | Chief Executive Officer |
| Continuity | Low | Duration of interruption of services to clients | Half a day | One business day | Chief Executive Officer |
| Complaints | Low | Complaints not dealt with within 30 days | 1 | 3 | Head of Sales |
