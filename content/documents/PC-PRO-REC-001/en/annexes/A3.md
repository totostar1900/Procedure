The parts in square brackets are to be completed. The tone remains simple, precise and courteous; no letter mentions any suspicion of money laundering.

**1. Acknowledgement of receipt** (within two business days)

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> We received your complaint concerning [subject] on [date]. It has been recorded under number [no.] and is being handled by [position]. You can follow its progress in your Guichet area.
>
> We will reply to you in writing no later than [deadline]. If we need any further information, we will contact you.
>
> Yours faithfully,

**2. Holding letter** (before the deadline, if the investigation takes longer)

> Your complaint no. [no.] requires checks with [third party] which have not yet been completed. We will reply to you no later than [new date]. We apologise for this delay.

**3. Reply: complaint upheld**

> We have examined your complaint no. [no.] of [date]. You informed us that [summary of the request].
>
> Our checks show that [findings, with supporting documents]. You are right: [acknowledgement of the error].
>
> We have therefore [redress: correction, refund of FCFA X, goodwill payment], effective on [date]. We have also [measure taken to prevent this from happening again].
>
> If you are not satisfied with this reply, you may tell us so and your complaint will be re-examined by [Chief Executive Officer / Chairman of the Board of Directors]. You may also refer the matter to the Central African Financial Market Supervisory Commission (COSUMAF).

**4. Reply: complaint not upheld** (reasoned refusal)

> We have examined your complaint no. [no.] of [date]. You informed us that [summary of the request].
>
> Our checks show that [precise findings, with supporting documents: order signed on…, executed at the price of…, in accordance with Article… of the agreement]. For this reason, we are unable to grant your request for [request].
>
> We understand that this reply may disappoint you. [Useful explanation for the future, where applicable.]
>
> If you are not satisfied with this reply, you may tell us so and your complaint will be re-examined by [Chief Executive Officer / Chairman of the Board of Directors]. You may also refer the matter to the Central African Financial Market Supervisory Commission (COSUMAF).

**5. Final position** (after re-examination)

> Your complaint no. [no.] has been re-examined by [position]. [Decision and reasons.] This reply constitutes the final position of Purpose Capital S.A. You retain the right to refer the matter to COSUMAF or to the competent courts.
