## Title I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

This policy sets out how Purpose Capital S.A. (the "Company") pays the people who work for it. Its aim is simple: that no one is paid for putting his or her own interests, or those of the Company, before those of clients, or for taking risks that the Company cannot bear.

Well-designed remuneration attracts and retains competent Staff Members, rewards work well done and protects the Company's reputation. Poorly designed remuneration drives people to sell anything to anyone. This policy upholds the former and prohibits the latter.

### Article 2 — Scope

The policy applies:

- to salaried **Staff Members**, whatever their contract and length of service, including paid interns;
- to the **Chief Executive Officer** and other senior managers, as regards its principles;
- to **directors**, as regards their fees;
- to **business introducers and service providers** paid according to the clients or transactions they bring in, as regards the principles of Article 12.

### Article 3 — Legal basis

The policy complies with the Cameroonian Labour Code and, if it applies to the Company, the national collective agreement for banks and financial institutions. It complies with the OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE) as regards the remuneration of senior managers and directors, and with COSUMAF regulations, which require firms to serve clients' best interests and to prevent conflicts of interest.

It adopts, on a scale suited to a small company, international good practice: the 2009 principles of the Financial Stability Board and the 2023 guidelines of the European Securities and Markets Authority on the remuneration of client-facing staff.

### Article 4 — Definitions

- **Fixed remuneration**: base salary, bonuses and allowances provided for by the contract, the law or the collective agreement, paid regardless of results.
- **Variable remuneration**: a bonus whose amount depends on the achievement of objectives or on results.
- **Benefits**: benefits in kind or in cash linked to employment (vehicle, housing, telephone, health insurance, training).
- **Client-facing staff**: any person who advises clients, presents products to them or receives their orders.
- **Control function**: the function performed by the RCCI (compliance, internal control, risk management, AML/CFT).

## Title II — Principles

### Article 5 — Six principles

1. **The client's interest comes first**: no remuneration rewards the sale of one product rather than another, or a volume of transactions unrelated to clients' needs.
2. **Fixed pay is the foundation**: it must be sufficient for everyone to live on their salary without depending on their bonus. The variable component remains a minority, capped share.
3. **How matters as much as how much**: the variable component takes into account the quality of service, compliance with the rules and complaints, not just volumes.
4. **Controllers do not depend on what they control**: the RCCI's remuneration does not depend on the results of the activities he or she controls.
5. **The Company does not promise what it cannot pay**: the variable pool depends on the Company's financial position and on compliance with its own funds requirements.
6. **Transparency**: before the start of the year, everyone knows the components of his or her remuneration, his or her objectives and how he or she will be assessed.

### Article 6 — Equality

For work of equal value, remuneration is equal, without distinction based on sex, origin, religion, opinion or trade union membership. Differences are explained by the role, qualifications, experience and performance, and are documented.

## Title III — Components

### Article 7 — Fixed remuneration

Fixed remuneration is determined by the role, qualifications, experience and the market, according to the grid in Appendix 1. It complies with statutory minimums and, where applicable, those of the collective agreement. It is reviewed every year; an increase is never automatic, except where the law or the collective agreement requires it.

The rules for calculating payroll, social security contributions and tax deductions are kept in the Company's payroll management system.

### Article 8 — Variable remuneration

A variable bonus may be paid each year. It is subject to the following rules:

- it is **capped** as a percentage of annual fixed pay, according to the job category set out in Appendix 1;
- it is based on **written objectives**, set at the beginning of the year on the form in Appendix 2, which combine:
  - **quantitative** criteria, for no more than 60% of the bonus: accounts opened and active clients, assets held, transactions completed, the Company's results;
  - **qualitative** criteria, for at least 40% of the bonus: quality of advice and of files, client satisfaction and complaints, compliance with procedures, results of controls, training completed, team spirit;
- **no criterion relates to the sale of a particular product, issuer or asset management company**;
- it is never a vested right: it is decided each year under the conditions of Title V.

### Article 9 — Benefits

Benefits are granted according to the role and the needs of the position, not according to sales performance. They are listed in the employment contract and in Appendix 1.

### Article 10 — No guaranteed remuneration

No bonus is guaranteed, except, on an exceptional basis, for the first year of a newly recruited Staff Member, and for that year only. Severance payments comply with the law and the contract; they do not reward failure.

## Title IV — Specific rules

### Article 11 — Client-facing staff

For Staff Members who advise clients or receive their orders:

- quantitative criteria relate to overall results (clients served, assets held, satisfaction), never to the commission generated by a given product;
- an incomplete client file, advice unsuited to the client's profile, a justified complaint or a breach of the order reception procedure reduces the bonus;
- an objective achieved through transactions unrelated to the client's interest is not taken into account.

Each year, the RCCI checks that the objectives of these Staff Members do not create a conflict of interest within the meaning of the Conflicts of Interest Management Policy (PC-POL-CI-001).

### Article 12 — Business introducers and service providers

A business introducer is paid only under a written contract approved by the Chief Executive Officer after obtaining the opinion of the RCCI. The introducer's remuneration is disclosed to the client where regulations so require. It is due only after the client has been properly onboarded in accordance with the Client Onboarding Procedure (PC-PRO-KYC-001), and never for a rejected client.

### Article 13 — Control function

The RCCI's remuneration:

- is essentially fixed;
- includes, where applicable, a capped bonus based on completion of the control plan, the quality of the RCCI's work and compliance with deadlines for submissions to COSUMAF, never on turnover, the result of a transaction or the opinion he or she gives;
- is set by the Chief Executive Officer after informing the Chairman of the Board of Directors, who may request that it be examined by the Board.

This rule applies Article 10 of the Internal Control and Compliance Charter (PC-COD-003).

### Article 14 — Chief Executive Officer and senior managers

The remuneration of the Chief Executive Officer is set by the Board of Directors, within the framework of his or her corporate office and letter of objectives. The variable component, if any, is based on written objectives that include compliance, risk control, and the quality of relations with COSUMAF and with clients, and not merely financial results. The Chief Executive Officer does not take part in the deliberation concerning him or her.

The remuneration of the other heads of department is set by the Chief Executive Officer, within the pool approved by the Board.

### Article 15 — Directors

Directors' fees are voted by the general meeting of shareholders. Any exceptional remuneration of a director for a particular assignment follows the regulated agreements procedure provided for by the OHADA Uniform Act.

## Title V — Decision and adjustment

### Article 16 — Annual pool

Each year, the Chief Executive Officer proposes to the Board of Directors the overall pool for pay increases and variable remuneration, in the light of:

- the Company's results and financial position;
- compliance with own funds requirements and with the limits set by the Risk Management Policy (PC-POL-RIS-001);
- the incidents, complaints and control findings of the year.

The pool may be reduced or cancelled if the Company is loss-making, if its own funds are under threat, or if it is subject to a COSUMAF sanction.

### Article 17 — Individual assessment

Each Staff Member has an annual appraisal interview using the form in Appendix 2. The line manager assesses the achievement of objectives. The RCCI gives an opinion on each Staff Member's compliance criteria before any bonus decision. Decisions are entered in the register in Appendix 4.

### Article 18 — Reduction or cancellation of the bonus

A Staff Member's variable bonus is reduced or cancelled where, during the year:

- he or she has breached the Code of Conduct, a procedure or the regulations;
- he or she has caused a significant loss, a serious justified complaint or a COSUMAF sanction;
- his or her results were obtained by means contrary to clients' interests.

Where such facts are discovered after payment, the Company may, to the extent permitted by law, take them into account in the bonus for subsequent years. These measures are without prejudice to disciplinary sanctions.

### Article 19 — Payment

The variable bonus is paid in a single instalment, after the financial statements for the year have been approved and the Board of Directors has decided on the pool. It is subject to social security contributions and tax under ordinary law.

## Title VI — Governance and final provisions

### Article 20 — Roles

| Body or function | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Board of Directors | Approves the policy and the annual pool; sets the remuneration of the Chief Executive Officer; is informed of the RCCI's remuneration |
| General meeting | Votes directors' fees |
| Chief Executive Officer | Proposes the policy and the pool; sets Staff Members' objectives and remuneration within the pool; keeps the register in Appendix 4 |
| RCCI | Checks each year, using Appendix 3, that the policy and objectives do not create incentives contrary to clients' interests; gives an opinion on compliance criteria; reports to the Board |
| Line managers | Set and assess objectives with each Staff Member |

### Article 21 — Transparency

This policy is given to every Staff Member. Each year, everyone receives in writing his or her objectives, the cap on his or her bonus and the criteria for his or her assessment. Individual amounts are confidential.

### Article 22 — Retention

Objective and assessment forms, the RCCI's opinions and the register of decisions are retained for ten years.

### Article 23 — Review and entry into force

The policy is reviewed each year by the Board of Directors, on the basis of a report by the RCCI. Any amendment is approved by the Board.

This policy enters into force on the date of its adoption by the Board of Directors, as indicated in the document control sheet.
