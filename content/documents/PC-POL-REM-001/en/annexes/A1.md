Proposed caps, to be decided by the Board of Directors. The cap is expressed as a percentage of annual gross fixed remuneration. The fixed pay ranges are completed by the Chief Executive Officer and comply with statutory minimums and, where applicable, those of the collective agreement.

| Category | Positions concerned | Annual fixed pay range (FCFA) | Cap on the variable component | Quantitative / qualitative weighting | Possible benefits | Set by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive management | Chief Executive Officer | Corporate office | Set by the Board (proposed: 30%) | 50 / 50, including compliance and risk | According to corporate office | Board of Directors |
| Heads of department | Head of Sales, Head of Capital Markets | | 30% | 60 / 40 at most | Telephone, health insurance, training | Chief Executive Officer, within the pool |
| Control function | RCCI | | 15% | 0 / 100: control plan, quality of work, regulatory deadlines | Telephone, health insurance, training, professional membership fees | Chief Executive Officer, after informing the Chairman of the Board |
| Client-facing staff | Client relationship managers, client-facing analysts | | 20% | 60 / 40 at most, with no product-specific criterion | Telephone, health insurance, training | Chief Executive Officer |
| Support functions | Accounting, administrative assistance | | 15% | 40 / 60 | Health insurance, training | Chief Executive Officer |
| Interns | Paid interns | Internship allowance | None | — | — | Chief Executive Officer |

**Common rules.** No criterion relates to a particular product, issuer or asset management company. The bonus is reduced or cancelled in the cases set out in Article 18. No guaranteed bonus, except in the first year following recruitment.
