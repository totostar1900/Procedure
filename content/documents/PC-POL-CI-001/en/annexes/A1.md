| No. | Situation | Interests involved | Risk to the client | Prevention measures | Responsible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Company arranges and places an issue, and its relationship managers offer it to their clients | The Company's arranging and placement commissions; the investor's interest | Recommendation unsuited to the client's profile, to ensure the success of the issue | Documented suitability test; written disclosure of the Company's role; allocation approved by the RCCI | Head of Capital Markets · Head of Sales |
| 2 | The Company advises an issuer and holds inside information about it | Issuer's mandate; clients who trade its securities | Use or leak of the information | Chinese wall; insider list; security placed on the restricted list | RCCI |
| 3 | Oversubscription of an issue placed by the Company | Clients among themselves; accounts of the Company and of Staff Members | Preferential allocation to certain subscribers | Allocation rule set in the information memorandum; related accounts served last | Head of Capital Markets · RCCI |
| 4 | Client orders and transactions for own or personal account in the same security | Company, Staff Members, clients | Own order executed first (front running) | Time-stamped chronological handling; prior authorisation of personal transactions; after-the-event control | Chief Executive Officer · RCCI |
| 5 | Distribution of CIS units remunerated by retrocession from the management company | Retrocession received; client's interest | Steering towards the best-remunerated product | Open, multi-provider architecture; prior disclosure of retrocessions to the client; suitability test | Head of Sales · RCCI |
| 6 | A director, senior manager or shareholder has interests in an issuer or a counterparty | Related person; Company; clients | Transaction terms biased in favour of the related person | Declaration of interests; prior referral to the RCCI; decision by the Board, with the interested person abstaining; related-party agreements | Board of Directors · RCCI |
| 7 | Variable remuneration linked to the volume of transactions | Staff Member; client | Excessive unnecessary trading (churning) | Qualitative and compliance criteria in the variable component; monitoring of account turnover rates | Chief Executive Officer |
| 8 | Combination of the compliance and internal audit functions | Control function assessing its own arrangements | Undetected control failure | Annual external review; direct report to the Board | Board of Directors |
| 9 | Operations function performed by the Chief Executive Officer | Management; clients | Choices favouring the Company in reconciliations or allocations | Dual approval outside the standard procedure; monthly control by the RCCI | Chief Executive Officer · RCCI |
| 10 | Gifts, invitations or benefits received from a client, an issuer or a service provider | Staff Member; third party | Decision influenced by the benefit received | Ceiling and declaration (Code, Art. 22); gifts register | RCCI |
| 11 | Outside activity or corporate office held by a Staff Member | Staff Member; Company; clients | Competition or use of the Company's information | Prior authorisation; annual declaration | Chief Executive Officer · RCCI |
| 12 | IT service provider with access to client data | Service provider; clients | Use of the data for other purposes | Contractual clauses; named and logged accounts; annual access review | Chief Executive Officer |
| 13 | Research or market commentary on an issuer that is a client of the Company | Business relationship with the issuer; readers | Biased analysis | Disclosure of the business relationship; ban on publication during a mandate | Head of Capital Markets · RCCI |
