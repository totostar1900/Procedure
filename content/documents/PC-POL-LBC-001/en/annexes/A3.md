A red flag is not proof. It requires an enhanced examination (Article 20) and, if doubt remains, a report to the AML/CFT Officer (Article 23).

| Category | Red flag | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client behaviour | Reluctance to provide information, inconsistent or doubtful documents | Address cannot be found, identity document recently issued without explanation |
| Client behaviour | Interest in internal procedures and reporting thresholds rather than in investments | Repeated questions about what triggers a check |
| Client behaviour | Client clearly acting on behalf of an undisclosed third party | Orders dictated by a person present at meetings |
| Funds | Large payments without investment, followed by a request for repayment | Deposit of 80 million, no orders for two months, request for a transfer to another country |
| Funds | Payments received from a third party or request for payment to a third party | Subscription paid by a company with no apparent connection to the client |
| Funds | Amounts split to fall just below a verification or reporting threshold | Several successive payments of 4.9 million |
| Funds | Funds originating from a high-risk country or unrelated to the client's situation | Transfers received from a FATF-listed jurisdiction for a resident client with no activity abroad |
| Transactions | Purchases and sales in quick succession with no economic rationale, acceptance of losses | Purchase of bonds followed by immediate resale at a discount |
| Transactions | Cross trades between linked accounts in an illiquid security | Two linked clients sell and buy back the same security from each other at rising prices |
| Transactions | Free-of-payment transfers of securities to persons with no apparent connection | Transfer of securities from a senior manager's account to that of a third party |
| Transactions | Unusual transactions ahead of a major announcement concerning an issuer | Massive purchase just before the announcement of an offer |
| Profile | Amounts out of proportion to declared income or wealth | Subscription of 300 million by an employee declaring FCFA 500,000 per month |
| Profile | PEP or close associate whose investments exceed known income | Regular purchases of government securities for large amounts |
| Structures | Shell companies, layered shareholdings, nominees | Recently incorporated company, with no activity, owned by another foreign company |
| Terrorist financing | Small regular amounts to or from conflict zones, non-profit organisation with poorly explained resources | Association whose donations come from unknown sources and which requests frequent withdrawals |
