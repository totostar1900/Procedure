**Documents to be obtained before client onboarding**

| Type of client | Identification | Additional supporting documents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural person | Valid national identity card or passport; date and place of birth; nationality | Proof of address less than three months old; occupation and employer; proof of income; tax identification number if available |
| Minor or protected adult | Identity document of the account holder | Identity document of the legal representative and document establishing his or her authority |
| Trading company | Trade and Personal Property Credit Register (RCCM) extract less than three months old; up-to-date articles of association; tax identification number | List of senior managers and their identity documents; minutes or power of attorney designating the signatories; ownership chart down to the natural persons; latest financial statements |
| Association, foundation, non-profit organisation | Articles of association; acknowledgement of registration or instrument of authorisation | List of managers; origin of resources (membership fees, donations, grants); intended use of funds |
| Collective investment scheme | Name, legal form, licence references, ISIN code | Identity and licence of the management company and of the depositary |
| State, local authority, public body | Instrument of establishment or organisation | Instrument authorising the signatories |
| Beneficial owner | Identity document; nature and percentage of ownership or control | Signed declaration by the client; extract from the register of beneficial owners where one exists |

**Functions that confer politically exposed person status**

- head of State, head of government, minister and secretary of State;
- member of parliament, member of a governing body of a political party;
- member of a supreme court, a constitutional court or a high court;
- member of a court of auditors or of the board of a central bank;
- ambassador, chargé d'affaires, general officer of the armed forces;
- senior manager or director of a State-owned enterprise;
- senior manager of an international or regional organisation (CEMAC, BEAC, COBAC, COSUMAF, BDEAC, United Nations bodies);
- regional governor, mayor of a large city, senior civil servant at director level or above in a financial, tax or customs administration.

The following are also treated as PEPs: the spouse or partner, children and their spouses, and parents; any person known to hold a business or joint interests with a PEP, or to be the beneficial owner of a structure set up for the benefit of a PEP.
