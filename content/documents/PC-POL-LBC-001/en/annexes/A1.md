Add up the points for each factor. **0 to 4 points: low risk · 5 to 9 points: standard risk · 10 points or more: high risk.** An "automatically high" box classifies the client as high risk regardless of the total.

| Factor | 0 points | 2 points | 4 points | Automatically high |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type of client | Financial institution or listed company in the CEMAC; State, public authority | Individual employee or retiree; trading company with known shareholders | Sole proprietorship; association, foundation or non-profit organisation; holding company | Structure whose beneficial owner cannot be clearly identified |
| Political exposure | None | — | Former holder of a prominent public function for more than twelve months | PEP, family member or person known to be a close associate |
| Activity or source of income | Salary, pension, regulated activity | Trade, liberal profession, agriculture | Extractive industries, logging, public procurement, real estate, manual currency exchange, virtual assets | — |
| Country of residence or origin of funds | Cameroon or another CEMAC country | Non-CEMAC country not listed by the FATF | Non-CEMAC country under increased FATF monitoring | Country subject to a FATF call for countermeasures or to international sanctions |
| Onboarding method | In person | Remotely with two independent verifications | Through a business introducer | — |
| Expected investment amount | Less than FCFA 25,000,000 per year | FCFA 25,000,000 to 250,000,000 per year | More than FCFA 250,000,000 per year | — |
| Services requested | Investments in listed securities or in collective investment schemes (OPC), custody | Subscriptions to primary issues, government securities | Free-of-payment transfers of securities, orders given by an authorised agent | — |
| History | No red flags | Unconfirmed adverse information in open sources | Confirmed adverse information | Previous suspicious transaction report |
