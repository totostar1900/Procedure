The calendar brings together everything that falls due on a fixed date. The details of each submission, its approval workflow and its register are set out in the Regulatory Reporting Procedure (PC-PRO-REP-001, Appendix 1). Deadlines marked "to be confirmed" must be checked with COSUMAF; late submission exposes the Company to a penalty of FCFA 250,000 per day (General Regulation, Art. 32).
