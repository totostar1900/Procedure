Indicative outline, based on sub-regional practice. To be adapted to any COSUMAF instruction on the content of the report. The report is short and factual, and each statement refers to evidence kept available.

| Part | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 1. The Company and its business | Significant events of the year; activities carried out and the share of each; changes in headcount, organisation, senior management and shareholding; changes submitted to COSUMAF |
| 2. The internal control framework | Organisation of the three lines; RCCI and professional card; risk and compliance committee (number of meetings); repository documents adopted or revised |
| 3. Risks | Updated risk map (attached); changes in the main risks; compliance with limits and limit breaches; own funds |
| 4. Controls performed | Control plan and completion rate; main findings by area; major findings and measures taken |
| 5. AML/CFT | Risk assessment; high-risk clients; alerts and suspicious transaction reports (number only); training |
| 6. Clients | Client assets and results of reconciliations; complaints (number, nature, response times); incidents affecting clients |
| 7. Systems, continuity and service providers | IT incidents; business continuity plan tests; outsourcing register; assessment of critical service providers |
| 8. External controls | Conclusions of the independent review, the statutory auditors and COSUMAF; status of their recommendations |
| 9. Follow-up of recommendations | Open, closed and overdue recommendations (Appendix 3) |
| 10. Assessment and programme | RCCI's assessment of the effectiveness of the framework; resources required; control plan for the following year |
| Appendices | Risk map; recommendations tracking table; any observations of the Board of Directors |
