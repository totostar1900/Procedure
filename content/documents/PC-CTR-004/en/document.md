**Preamble**

The Account Keeper is a brokerage firm (société de bourse) which, on behalf of its Clients, keeps accounts and holds securities in custody and/or, drawing on its experience, assists them in making decisions on investment matters.

The Client is an individual interested in securities account keeping services.

Accordingly, the parties have come together to settle the terms, conditions and arrangements of this agreement (hereinafter, the "Agreement").

## Title I — Purpose and opening of the account

### Article 1 — Purpose of the Agreement and applicable regulations

#### 1.1 Purpose of the Agreement

The purpose of this securities account agreement (hereinafter "the Agreement") is to govern the relations between Purpose Capital (hereinafter "the Account Keeper") and the designated holder(s) (hereinafter "the Client") relating to the opening and operation of a securities account.

#### 1.2 Applicable regulations

The Agreement is governed by Ministerial Regulation No. 01/22/CEMAC/UMAC/CM/COSUMAF of 21 July 2022 on the organisation and functioning of the Central African Financial Market, and by the General Regulation of the Central Africa Financial Market Supervisory Commission (COSUMAF) of 23 May 2023.

The Agreement takes into account Articles 3 et seq. of COSUMAF Instruction No. 02-10 of 28 April 2010 on the securities account opening agreement, which specify the mandatory clauses to be included in the account opening agreement between a securities account keeper and its Client, and COSUMAF Instruction No. 03-15 of 10 December 2015 setting out the specifications for custodian account keepers.

The Agreement also takes into account the details provided by:

- Decree No. 2014/3763/PM of 13 November 2014 setting the conditions for the application of Law No. 2014/007 of 23 April 2014 laying down the arrangements for the dematerialisation of securities in Cameroon;
- Regulation 01/14/CEMAC-UMAC-CM/ of 25 April 2014 establishing a book-entry registration regime for securities and other financial instruments in the CEMAC;
- Regulation No. 04/CEMAC/UMAC/CM on the CEMAC market in negotiable debt securities;
- Decree No. 2007/0457/PM of 4 April 2007 amending and supplementing certain provisions of Decree No. 94/611/PM of 30 December 1994 regulating the issue and management of Negotiable Government Securities;
- and all other regulations in force.

### Article 2 — Conditions for opening and operating the securities account

#### 2.1 Definitions

**The securities account** is an account that receives deposits of securities or transferable securities in dematerialised form. It is subject to specific organisation and accounting and is indispensable to any investor wishing to carry out transactions in transferable securities or on the financial market. It records securities transactions. The securities account must be linked to an account with a bank or a payment institution. This information will be provided by the Client.

Account keeping consists, first, in recording in the Account Keeper's books the entries accounting for the various movements and transactions in transferable securities and, second, in holding and administering those securities on behalf of the Client.

#### 2.2 General conditions for opening an account

Any natural person with legal capacity, or duly represented, may be the holder of a securities account. Nevertheless, PURPOSE CAPITAL refrains from opening an anonymous securities account and remains free to refuse to open a securities account without having to give reasons for its decision.

The Account Keeper opens a securities account after carrying out the necessary checks in accordance with the legal and regulatory provisions in force. The Client's identity and place of residence are verified on presentation of a valid official identity document bearing a signature (with photograph) and of proof of residence dated less than 3 months.

Where applicable, the official documents evidencing the powers of the Client's legal or judicial representative(s) must be provided before the securities account is opened.

The opening of the securities account is accompanied by the lodging with the Account Keeper of a specimen signature of the account holder(s) and of the persons authorised to operate it.

In addition to the above, opening the securities account requires the following to be provided:

- A 4x4 photograph;
- A location plan of the place of residence;
- A tax registration certificate;
- A bank account details statement.

The above provisions apply to all Co-holders of a collective account. Any attorney or representative appointed to operate the account must also prove their identity and place of residence on the same terms.

The Account Keeper keeps the references or a copy of the various documents presented.

The securities account is necessarily associated with a cash account (current account) in the name of the securities account holder or of one of the co-holders where applicable, on which the credits and debits corresponding to the transactions carried out on the securities account are recorded. A Client who opens a securities account expressly acknowledges having read and accepted the Account Keeper's conditions.

The securities account may be an individual account, a joint account, an undivided account or an account in usufruct and bare ownership, as defined in Article 2.3 below.

The Account Keeper must also be informed of any change in the Client's situation (change of civil status, matrimonial regime, legal capacity, etc.) and of any matter likely to affect the operation of the securities account (address, telephone numbers, etc.). Failing such information, the Account Keeper will regard the information it holds as accurate.

The identification details of the securities account appear on the securities account opening form.

#### 2.3 Specific provisions for certain types of securities account

#### 2.3.1 Joint securities account

A joint securities account allows each Co-holder, separately, to carry out any transaction on that securities account.

The Co-holders are jointly and severally liable towards the Account Keeper for the performance of all commitments bearing the signature of any one of them.

Without the agreement of the other Co-holder(s), the joint securities account arrangement may be ended by one of the Co-holders withdrawing from joint liability, by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt addressed to the Account Keeper. The effect of this withdrawal is that, after receipt of the registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, the joint securities account is converted into an undivided securities account (see below).

Each Co-holder nevertheless remains jointly and severally liable for transactions carried out before this withdrawal.

A joint securities account may not be opened with a legal person, an unemancipated minor or a protected adult.

#### 2.3.2 Undivided securities account

An undivided securities account is an account opened in the name of several Co-owners in undivided ownership, which requires, both for its opening and for its operation, the joint signature of all the Co-owners, unless a power of attorney has been given to one of them or to a third party, or reciprocal mandates exist.

The Co-owners in undivided ownership are jointly and severally liable for their commitments towards PURPOSE CAPITAL.

#### 2.3.3 Securities account in usufruct and bare ownership

A securities account in bare ownership is an account in which ownership is dismembered: the capital belongs to the bare owner and the income from the securities is paid into a cash account opened in the name of the usufructuary.

Orders and acts of disposal (in particular buy and sell orders or transfer orders) must be signed by the usufructuary and the bare owner, unless a power of attorney has been given to one of them or reciprocal mandates exist. Acts of day-to-day administration may be performed on the usufructuary's initiative alone.

Only the bare owner, as shareholder, may exercise the rights attached to the status of shareholder (in particular exercising the option to receive dividends in shares proposed by the shareholders' meeting). The usufructuary is solely responsible for notifying the bare owner so as to enable them to exercise their rights.

The usufructuary receives the dividend and the fruits of the securities.

In the event of sale, redemption or amortisation of the securities, the capital is paid into the bare owner's cash account; the bare owner has full responsibility for reinvesting the sums arising from the securities sold, redeemed or amortised.

#### 2.3.4 Securities account of minors

The opening of a securities account in the name of a minor and transactions on the securities account are carried out by the legal representative(s), who alone are responsible for the proper operation of the account.

#### 2.3.5 Securities account of protected adults

The opening and operation of securities accounts opened for protected adults are subject to the protection regime ordered by the judicial authority (judicial safeguard, statutory administration under judicial supervision, curatorship, guardianship, etc.).

The securities account may be opened only on presentation of the order of the guardianship judge and on the conditions defined in the court decision. The account will accordingly operate under the protection regime ordered and in accordance with the arrangements set by the judicial authority.

If a protection measure arises during the life of the securities account, it is for the representative of the Client, who has become a protected adult, to inform the account keeper of the protection measure on presentation of the supporting document (copy of the court decision).

#### 2.4 Administered registered securities

Administered registered securities are individualised financial securities entered in the registers of the issuing company in the name of their holder, who entrusts their administration to their usual financial intermediary.

To this end, the account holder(s) hereby give(s) PURPOSE CAPITAL, which accepts, a mandate to administer their registered financial securities whose entries appear in the accounts held with the issuers and will be reproduced in their securities account(s) opened in the books of PURPOSE CAPITAL.

By virtue of this mandate, the account keeper will perform all acts of administration on behalf of the Client(s), in particular the collection of proceeds (income, dividends, etc.).

By contrast, acts of disposal, in particular the exercise of rights in capital increases, and settlements of securities or cash, are carried out on the express and prior instruction of the Client(s). The Client(s) may rely on their tacit acceptance for certain transactions in accordance with prevailing practice. The account keeper notifies the Client(s) of transactions affecting the securities account. Portfolio statements and transaction reports are sent in accordance with the arrangements laid down in Article 11 "Information" of this Agreement.

The pecuniary rights (dividends, allotment of bonus shares, etc.) attached to administered registered securities may be exercised by any of the Co-holders in the case of a joint securities account.

The non-pecuniary rights (attendance and voting at general meetings of shareholders, etc.) attached to these financial securities are exercised by the Co-holder named first in the title of the securities account.

In the case of financial securities that are registered directly with the issuer, the Client acknowledges having been informed of the risks, in particular those linked to the issuer's improper execution of instructions on these securities, to potential difficulties in recognising the Client's rights, for which the Account Keeper cannot be held liable, and to valuation errors concerning these securities.

The administration mandate may be terminated at any time and without notice by either party, by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, and takes effect on receipt of the letter.

Such termination entails the immediate closure of the securities account.

#### 2.5 Power of attorney

The Client may, in writing and for an indefinite period, give a power of attorney to one or more natural persons with legal capacity who are not subject to a banking or judicial prohibition (hereinafter the "Attorney") to carry out, in the Client's name, on the Client's behalf and under the Client's full responsibility, any transaction on the Client's securities account, except closure.

The Client undertakes to inform the Attorney(s) personally of the terms of the Agreement.

The power of attorney will be given by a separate instrument specifying the nature of the transactions that the attorney may carry out. In the case of a joint securities account, the signature of all the Co-holders will be required.

In the latter case, the person(s) chosen must also be authorised to operate the associated cash account.

The Attorney appointed must prove their identity and place of residence and, for that purpose, present:

- a valid official identity document that serves as proof and bears their photograph,
- proof of residence dated less than 3 months,
- and lodge a specimen of their signature and be approved by the account keeper.

If approval of the Attorney is refused, the Account Keeper will inform the Client in writing and by any means as soon as possible.

Transactions carried out by the Attorney within the powers entrusted to them by the Client engage the Client's full responsibility.

The Attorney of a Collective account must be appointed by mutual agreement, in writing, by all the Co-holders of the securities account.

This power of attorney ends, whether the securities account is individual or collective, in the following cases:

- revocation of the power of attorney by the Client,
- renunciation of the power of attorney by the Attorney,
- death or incapacity of the Client or of the Attorney,
- change of one of the holders of a joint or undivided account, or conversion into an individual account,
- closure of the securities account.

In all cases, the Client or, where applicable, the Attorney must notify the Account Keeper of the occurrence of any of the situations listed above, by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt.

Revocation or renunciation takes effect on the date the letter is received by the managing post office or the financial centre, subject to transactions in progress.

A Client who revokes a power of attorney in writing must inform the Attorney at the same time and, where applicable, undertakes to provide PURPOSE CAPITAL with proof of having done so.

### Article 3 — Categorisation and classification of clients

#### 3.1 Categorisation and change of category of Clients

The Client is informed by the Account Keeper, in the securities account domiciliation certificate, of the Client's categorisation as a "Qualified investor" or a "Non-qualified investor".

#### 3.1.1 Definition of client categories

The COSUMAF General Regulation distinguishes two main categories of Clients:

#### 3.1.1.1 Clients known as "Non-qualified investors"

These Clients benefit from the highest level of protection under the legal and regulatory provisions.

#### 3.1.1.2 Clients known as "Qualified investors"

A qualified investor means a person or entity having the skills and means necessary to understand the risks inherent in transactions in transferable securities or other investment products.

They have the experience, knowledge and competence necessary to make their own investment decisions and to assess properly the risks incurred.

Article 89 of Ministerial Regulation No. 01/22/CEMAC/UMAC/CM/COSUMAF of 21 July 2022 on the organisation and functioning of the Central African Financial Market provides that:

The following in particular are regarded as qualified investors:

- credit institutions and other licensed or regulated intermediaries or financial institutions;
- collective investment undertakings and their management companies;
- insurance and reinsurance undertakings;
- insurance group companies;
- mutual societies and unions of mutual societies;
- provident institutions;
- pension funds and other public or private bodies that take deposits and manage funds;
- development finance institutions;
- brokerage firms (sociétés de bourse) and other licensed market intermediaries;
- investment funds.

Clients known as "Qualified investors" are presumed to have the experience and knowledge necessary to understand the risks inherent in the instruments, transactions or services for which they are so categorised.

#### 3.1.2 Change of category

The Client undertakes to inform PURPOSE CAPITAL of any change likely to alter the Client's categorisation. The Client may request a change of category, either to move to a more protective regime or to waive some of the protections from which they benefit. The request for a change of category must be sent by letter to the Account Keeper, which reserves the right to refuse it.

#### 3.2 Classification of Clients

Having regard to the information given to it by the Client, in particular on the Client's financial situation, experience and financial knowledge, the account keeper determines the classification into which the Client falls.

In the absence of information on the Client's financial situation, objectives, experience and financial knowledge, the account keeper must refrain from providing the investment advice service and automatically categorises the Client as a "Non-qualified investor". Where the service of reception and transmission of orders on behalf of third parties is provided, the Account Keeper informs the Client that it is not required to assess the appropriateness of the service or of the financial instrument. An order transmitted by the Client on the Client's own initiative is treated as execution only.

## Title II — Securities, markets and orders

### Article 4 — Classification and form of financial securities that may be registered in an account

#### 4.1 Classification of financial securities

within the meaning of Article 6 of Ministerial Regulation No. 01/22/CEMAC/UMAC/CM/COSUMAF of 21 July 2022 on the organisation and functioning of the Central African Financial Market:

These are mainly:

- transferable securities;
- negotiable government securities arising from the securitisation of the domestic debt of one or more CEMAC Member States;
- negotiable debt securities supervised by the BEAC;
- Sukuks and similar financial securities.

#### 4.2 Form of financial securities

Financial securities take the form of either bearer securities or registered securities.

In accordance with Article 7 of Regulation 01/14/CEMAC-UMAC-CM/ of 25 April 2014, where securities or financial instruments are entered in a securities account kept by a licensed financial intermediary, they are said to be "bearer" securities.

In accordance with Article 8 of Regulation 01/14/CEMAC-UMAC-CM/ of 25 April 2014, where securities or financial instruments are entered in a securities account kept by the issuing legal person, they are said to be "registered" securities.

Registered securities administered by the issuing legal persons are said to be "pure registered".

Registered securities administered, by virtue of a mandate, by a licensed financial intermediary are said to be "administered registered".

### Article 5 — The markets

The Account Keeper carries out transactions in financial securities (equities and bonds market) admitted to trading on the Central African Financial Market, on the CEMAC Government Securities Market and on the private market (negotiable debt securities and registered shares).

### Article 6 — Orders

Stock exchange orders and transactions in collective investment schemes (OPCVM) are expressed in CFA francs.

#### 6.1 Cover for orders

A buy order is valid, that is to say accepted by the "account keeper", only if the "Client" has first built up in the cash account with PURPOSE CAPITAL a cash provision sufficient to cover the transaction envisaged.

This provision depends on the quantities requested and the unit price of the securities requested.

The "Client" may sell only the Securities that the Client actually holds with a good value date, and may buy securities only up to the Client's authorisation limit or the cash provision that the Client has previously built up.

If there is no provision, the "Account Keeper" cannot be held to account for failing to process and execute the order. Any order duly recorded and executed by the "Account Keeper" may no longer be challenged by the "Client".

#### 6.2 Mandatory particulars on the "Client's" orders

Any order to buy or sell securities must include the following information, on pain of nullity:

- the designation of the security that is the subject of the transaction,
- the number of securities,
- the security that is the subject of the transaction,
- the direction of the transaction,
- the price or the price range,
- the period of validity of the order,
- the full identity of the person placing the order,
- the date on which the order is placed,
- the Client's signature,
- the cash and securities account numbers of the "Client".

#### 6.3 Types of order

#### 6.3.1 The "limit" order

This is an order by which the person placing the order (the Client), as buyer, sets the maximum price that they are willing to pay or by which the person placing the order, as seller, sets the minimum price at which they agree to sell their securities. This type of order has the advantage of allowing control over the execution price, but the person placing the order has no guarantee that the order will be executed in full. This type of order may indeed be executed in part, or not executed at all.

#### 6.3.2 The "market" order

It carries no limit and may be executed in part. A market order is executed up to the maximum quantity immediately available, with the balance remaining in the order book. If a market order finds no counterparty, it also remains in the order book until it is executed or cancelled, either by the Client or because its validity limit has been reached. During the trading session, the "market" order is executed in full or in part according to the possibilities offered on the market sheet.

#### 6.4 Price of the order

The price of the order is expressed per unit or as a percentage of par value.

#### 6.5 Size of the order

The volume of an order is expressed as a quantity of securities or as an amount.

#### 6.6 Orders on securities

All orders to buy or sell securities placed with the Account Keeper are traded for cash and executed in accordance with the provisions of the full prospectus or, failing that, the information memorandum.

The simplified prospectus or the information memorandum is handed over before any subscription.

### Article 7 — Transmission of orders

#### 7.1 Arrangements for transmitting orders

The "Client" holding a securities account may place buy or sell orders at the counters of the "account keeper". To do so, the Client must complete the "order form" made available by the Account Keeper and sign it. The Client may opt for any other means of transmission made available by the account keeper (internet, telephone via online services, subject to authentication).

The Client bears responsibility for any means of transmitting orders agreed with the account keeper and releases the account keeper from the consequences that may result from the use of the means of communication chosen by the Client to transmit the order.

Orders by telephone are accepted if they are placed from a private line whose number is given to the "Account Keeper" when this agreement is signed. To that end, the "Client" must always state this number for each order placed by telephone with the account keeper. Orders placed by telephone must be confirmed by an order form duly completed and signed by the "Client".

The Account Keeper may at any time cease to accept certain means of placing orders, provided that it has first informed the Client by any means, in particular where a dispute has arisen over those means of transmission. The Client is expressly informed that the transmission of the order with a view to its execution does not prejudge its execution. The account keeper will not accept orders whose conditions of execution would not comply with the regulations in force or would prove incompatible with market conditions.

The Client releases the Account Keeper from the consequences that may result from the use of any means of transmitting orders other than those provided for in the Agreement.

The Account Keeper may set intervention limits according to the nature of the transaction and for each type of financial security and market concerned. These limits are enforceable against the Client as of right. Any order transmitted that would exceed these limits may not be executed. Where the order could not be transmitted, the Account Keeper will use its best efforts to inform the Client. An order that could not be transmitted is deemed to have expired. It is for the Client to place a new order, where appropriate.

The order may be executed in part only. Failing express instructions from the Client, any partial execution once begun binds the Client.

Where a Client's order is unusual by reason of its nature, its terms, the market or its amount, the Account Keeper, before processing and executing the order, will warn the Client of this, and will provide the Client with the information useful for understanding the transaction envisaged and the risks attached to it. Having read this information, the Client may then confirm the order. The Client may transmit orders on the Client's own initiative on an execution-only basis.

Each order must be duly completed with all the fields necessary for its transmission and its proper execution on the market, as specified in Article 5.2 of this agreement.

It must also be dated and signed by the Client (or the Client's legal representative or Attorney) before it is transmitted to the market.

In the absence of these fields, the Account Keeper is not required to transmit the order. Any order that has been executed cannot be cancelled.

Given the uncertainties that may arise when orders are transmitted by post or by fax, the Account Keeper cannot be held liable for the failure to execute, or the late execution of, orders transmitted.

The order is sent to the account keeper under the Client's sole responsibility. The Client's attention is specifically drawn to the possibility of a delay, of unpredictable length, between the moment at which the Client issues the order and the moment at which PURPOSE CAPITAL receives it.

In all cases, the evidence showing that these orders were placed by the Client and that they match the account keeper's entries is authoritative.

The Account Keeper reserves the right, in the Client's interest and where it is not acting as placing institution, to refuse the reception and transmission of orders for certain bond issues on the primary market.

The Client has the right to request the cancellation of the order after its transmission. This request may, however, be taken into account only to the extent that the order has not been executed. The Account Keeper will process this cancellation request as soon as possible. Cancellation charges will be borne by the Client. Any order executed despite a late cancellation request will be entered in the Client's securities account.

#### 7.2 Period of validity of orders

a) "day" validity: in this case, the order may be executed only during the current trading day and will automatically be withdrawn from the market if it has not been executed.

b) "specified date" validity: the order remains on the market, as long as it has not been executed and until the date indicated by the person placing the order. This date may not exceed 365 calendar days.

If the person placing the order omits to indicate a validity, "day" validity is applied automatically to the order.

If an order has not been executed on the day its validity expires, a new order is needed to renew it even if the conditions of execution are identical.

Where a subscription or allotment right is detached, unexecuted orders are automatically withdrawn from the market and must therefore be renewed by the persons placing the orders. The same applies, with exceptions, in the case of suspensions of trading. When a dividend is detached, unexecuted orders remain on the market but their limit is lowered by the amount of the net dividend.

## Title III — Custody, responsibilities and information

### Article 8 — Custody of securities

As custodian account keeper, PURPOSE CAPITAL safeguards all the financial securities entered in the account and, for that purpose, in particular collects dividends or coupons and handles the amortisation or redemption of financial securities.

The account keeper will hold and return the financial securities deposited on the Client's simple request, subject to unavailability arising from their being pledged as security (pledge, etc.) or from rights that third parties may assert.

Financial securities entered in the account may not be used by the Account Keeper without the Client's prior and express agreement.

For the financial securities it holds in custody, the Account Keeper undertakes to comply with the market rules on the custody of securities, in particular those defined in COSUMAF Instruction No. 03-15 of 10 December 2015 setting out the specifications for custodian account keepers.

For foreign financial securities traded on foreign markets, the Account Keeper is expressly authorised by the Client to provide the foreign custodians with all useful information. The Account Keeper nevertheless reserves the right to refuse the entry in the account and the trading of financial securities issued and held abroad.

### Article 9 — Responsibilities of the Account Keeper

The Account Keeper acts in accordance with the customs, rules of the art and practices of the brokerage firm profession, in compliance with the laws and regulations in force.

The Account Keeper is subject only to an obligation of means and its liability may be engaged only on the basis of a serious fault proved by the Client. Only direct damage suffered by the Client may then give rise to possible compensation. The Account Keeper is not liable for lost opportunities that may result from the Client's choices or omissions. Under this Agreement, the Account Keeper's liability may not be engaged in the following cases, without this list being exhaustive:

- any event amounting to force majeure,
- any operating incident at the places of execution, industrial dispute, strike or suspension of trading,
- the failure of the market undertaking on which the Account Keeper acts at the Client's request.

### Article 10 — Risks arising from transactions in financial securities

The Client acknowledges having read the regulations and the functioning of the markets on which the Client carries out transactions. The Client also acknowledges being aware of the risks inherent in these transactions, of their speculative nature and of the risks of loss of the capital invested.

The Client also acknowledges that the Client must keep personally informed of any event affecting the life of any company issuing securities held on deposit and likely to influence the value of those securities, such as, for example, the reorganisation or judicial liquidation of the issuing company; the Account Keeper assumes no obligation to provide information in this respect.

### Article 11 — Information

- Frequency of securities account statements: the "Account Keeper" will inform the "Client" or the Client's attorney of the position of the Client's portfolio by sending a monthly account statement summarising all the transactions and movements that have taken place on the account during the month.
- Information on the execution of orders: for each transaction validly executed, the "Account Keeper" sends the "Client" or the Client's attorney, no later than two (2) business days after the actual settlement of the transaction, a "contract note" (avis d'opéré) informing the Client of the execution of the order and of the terms of that execution.
- Information on allocations of securities following a subscription on the primary market is notified to the "Client" or the Client's attorney by a contract note in the case of the money market, and by a certificate of ownership of securities for other markets.

Where the securities account is opened in the name of several holders (undivided securities account, account held jointly between third parties), transaction notices and portfolio statements are sent to the first co-holder named in the title of the securities account or to the person designated to receive this information.

If the Client does not receive these documents within the usual time, it is for the Client to inform the Account Keeper as soon as possible.

## Title IV — Miscellaneous provisions

### Article 12 — Other provisions

The Account Keeper asks the Client for all instructions useful for completing the optional formalities relating to the Client's pecuniary rights. Failing precise instructions within the required time, the Account Keeper is authorised to take any measures in the Client's interest, in particular the sale of those rights on the stock exchange, without these measures engaging the Account Keeper's liability. In all cases, the Account Keeper will act under an obligation of means, in the primary interest of its Clients.

#### 12.1 Transfer of the securities account

The Client may request the transfer of the Client's securities account to another account keeper. The transfer is carried out as soon as any sum owed to the account keeper under these presents has been duly paid.

Transfer charges are borne by the Client.

It is expressly agreed that, during the transfer period, the Client may not give any order to buy (subscribe for) or sell (redeem) securities.

#### 12.2 Complaints and disputes

Complaints concerning a stock exchange or OPCVM order must be made in writing to the Account Keeper, with acknowledgement of receipt, within two days of receipt of the transaction notice. After that time, the transaction is presumed to have been accepted by the Client.

Any other complaint may be accepted only in writing, within one month of receipt of the portfolio statement.

### Article 13 — Charges

The "Client" is informed that the management of the securities account is subject to charges.

Custody fees will be charged to the Client when income is received on each security. These custody fees are calculated per security, on an annual basis of 365 days, according to the volume of securities held, and pro rata to the time the security has been in the portfolio.

The placing of stock exchange orders gives rise to a commission payable to the Account Keeper, calculated on the volume of the transaction envisaged.

The Client declares having read the Account Keeper's schedule of charges annexed to this agreement and authorises the Account Keeper to debit the Client's account with all the fees and commissions, whatever their nature, appearing in the schedule of charges.

Any change in charges will be brought to the Client's attention beforehand.

### Article 14 — Taxation

The tax applicable to the income from the Client's securities portfolio is that of the Client's country of residence.

### Article 15 — Obligations of Purpose Capital

#### 15.1 Professional secrecy

The Account Keeper is bound by professional secrecy. However, this secrecy may be lifted in the cases provided for by law, in particular with regard to supervisory authorities, the tax administration and criminal authorities.

Likewise, in matters of AML/CFT, the Account Keeper is required to pass on to the companies of the group to which it belongs information covered by professional secrecy.

By express agreement, the Client authorises PURPOSE CAPITAL to disclose any useful information concerning the Client to any natural or legal person contributing to the performance of the services provided for by the Agreement or which might subsequently be linked to it, in particular to service providers for the performance of subcontracted work and/or to group companies for use in studying and managing files, in commercial prospecting and/or in other statistical studies.

#### 15.2 Anti-money laundering and countering the financing of terrorism

The account keeper is subject to a duty of due diligence and of information, by reason of the laws and regulations on AML/CFT.

#### 15.3 Monitoring of transactions in financial securities

The Account Keeper is subject to a duty of due diligence and of information, in application of the laws and regulations on money laundering and terrorist financing in Central Africa. In particular, the Account Keeper must report to the competent Authorities Client transactions that may be indicative of an intention to launder money, in accordance with Article 106 of the General Regulation of the Central Africa Financial Market Supervisory Commission (COSUMAF) of 23 May 2023.

### Article 16 — Amendment of the agreement

Changes in legislation or regulations may modify the clauses of the Agreement. In that case, the amendments will take effect on the date those measures apply, without any particular action by the Account Keeper towards the Client, unless specific legal or regulatory provisions provide otherwise.

The Account Keeper also reserves the right to amend clauses, to add to them or to delete them. Clauses of the Agreement amended on the Account Keeper's sole initiative will be brought to the Client's attention beforehand, by any means, two months before their entry into force.

Acceptance of these new conditions will result from the continued operation of the securities account and the Client's continuation, without reservation, of the relationship with the Account Keeper.

### Article 17 — Term and termination of the agreement

This Agreement is entered into for an indefinite term. It may be terminated at any time by either party on the conditions set out below.

In any event, if the Client or the Account Keeper fails to perform its commitments, the Agreement may be terminated as of right, without formal notice, on the initiative of the other party.

Termination of the Agreement entails as of right the closure of the securities account and the termination of the services attached to it.

However, orders or transactions in progress on that date will be executed in accordance with the Agreement.

The Client is nevertheless advised that any request to close a securities account (whether initiated by the Client or by the Account Keeper) entails the blocking of the securities account concerned, which means the Client can no longer place new orders.

On termination of the Agreement, the Client must give PURPOSE CAPITAL the details of the securities account to which the securities are to be transferred.

#### 17.1 Termination on the initiative of the Account Keeper

Termination by the Account Keeper will be effected on thirty calendar days' notice and the prior sending of a registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt.

A securities account holding no securities for a period of one year is closed without notice.

#### 17.2 Termination on the initiative of the Client

Termination takes effect on receipt of the Client's written request. The financial securities will either be liquidated on the Client's instruction or transferred to the securities account that the Client has indicated.

#### 17.3 Death of the Client

The death of the Client holding an individual securities account does not entail the closure of the securities account but its blocking.

Such blocking takes effect on receipt of a death notice or of any credible information attesting to the death of the securities account holder.

The securities account is closed only after the Account Keeper has received formal instructions from the persons entitled or their legal representatives at the end of the winding-up of the estate.

The heirs, legatees or any person authorised to act for the estate must provide the Account Keeper with all the documents required to establish their status and their rights.

The Account Keeper cannot be held liable for the tax consequences arising from the succession.

#### 17.3.1 Special case of the death of one of the co-holders of a joint securities account

In the event of the death of one of the co-holders, the securities account will not be blocked; it will continue to operate under the signature of the survivor or of any one of the survivors. Although active joint and several entitlement allows the survivor, on the death of one of the co-holders, to take hold of the assets in the securities account, it should be made clear that the survivor (or survivors) is (are) solely accountable for those assets towards the heirs of the deceased or their notary and must render account to them.

The surviving co-holder may exercise the non-pecuniary rights attached to the securities only if they are the first named in the title of the securities account or if they have been specially designated for that purpose.

Written opposition to the continued operation of the securities account under the signature of any one of the survivors, from a person entitled under the deceased holder who proves their status or from the notary in charge of the estate, will have the same effects as withdrawal from joint liability, and the securities account will be converted into an undivided securities account.

#### 17.3.2 Case of an undivided securities account

The death of one of the co-owners in undivided ownership entails the blocking of the account. The account is unblocked at the end of the winding-up of the estate.

In all cases, the co-owners in undivided ownership must maintain to the credit of their associated cash account a sufficient and available provision enabling the settlement of debit transactions in progress.

The Agreement is subject to OHADA law and to CEMAC regulations, and the language of communication between the Client and PURPOSE CAPITAL, as well as that used in the documents and information communicated to the Client, is French, including pre-contractual information.

All disputes arising from the interpretation of this contract will be decided by the courts within whose jurisdiction the Account Keeper has its registered office.
