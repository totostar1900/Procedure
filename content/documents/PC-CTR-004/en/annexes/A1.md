Amounts in CFA francs, before tax. Everything that is charged appears here, and so does everything that is not charged. The rates are those of the scale in force in the Guichet on the date of delivery; empty cells are completed by the adviser.

**Per transaction**

| What the Holder does | What Purpose Capital charges | What the product charges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invest in a fund | | The fund's subscription fees, shown on its product sheet |
| Exit a fund | | The fund's redemption fees, if any |
| Switch from one fund to another | | Redemption fees of the first, then subscription fees of the second |
| Auctions and issues | | None |
| Buy and sell on the stock exchange | | BVMAC market fees, if any are applied |

**Over time**

| What Purpose Capital provides | What it costs | How it is calculated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custody of securities | | Line by line, pro rata to the days actually held; the notice arrives before any debit |

**What is not charged**

- Opening of the securities account, and of the sub-account with the account keeper
- Payment of the balance to the declared bank account
- Direct debit mandate, and each direct debit presented
- Regular savings plan, and each of its advance notices
- Subscription forms, contract notes, statements, certificates and duplicates
- Closure of the account
