## Title I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

This procedure sets out what Purpose Capital S.A. (the "Company") keeps, for how long, on what medium, where and in whose custody. It also sets out how the Company proves that its archives have not been altered, how it provides them to the authorities, and how it destroys them when they no longer need to be kept.

Proper retention serves three purposes: to **prove** what the Company and its clients have done, to **respond** without delay to COSUMAF, ANIF, the statutory auditor or the courts, and to **protect** individuals' data by not keeping it longer than necessary.

### Article 2 — Scope

The procedure applies to all documents and data of the Company, whatever their medium: paper, files, email, Guichet data, recordings. It also applies to documents held on its behalf by its service providers.

### Article 3 — Definitions

- **Archive**: a document or data item that is no longer being processed but must be kept.
- **Retention period**: the minimum period for which an archive must be kept, counted from the event indicated in Appendix 1 (end of the relationship, date of the transaction, close of the financial year).
- **Original**: a document bearing the handwritten signature of a party, or an electronic document signed or time-stamped in the system that produced it.
- **Freeze**: suspension of any destruction of an archive, even when its period has expired, because of a dispute, an investigation or a request from an authority.
- **Archive owner**: the function that produces the category of documents and is responsible for its retention (Appendix 1).

### Article 4 — Who does what

| Function | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Chief Executive Officer | Owner of the procedure; decides on destruction; keeps the corporate records and the original contracts |
| RCCI | Maintains the table of retention periods and the register of freezes; monitors application of the procedure; keeps the compliance and AML/CFT files |
| Desk (operations) | Archives client files, orders and settlement records, in the Guichet and, for paper, in the originals cabinet |
| Accounting | Archives the accounting books and records |
| IT service provider | Applies the access, backup and destruction rules on the systems it administers, in accordance with its contract |
| Every Staff Member | Files their documents in the filing plan in Appendix 2; keeps no Company document on a personal device or medium |

## Title II — What the Company keeps, and for how long

### Article 5 — Retention periods

The retention periods are set in Appendix 1. The main ones are:

| Category | Period | Starting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client files, identity documents, screenings, review sheets | 10 years | End of the relationship |
| Orders, subscription forms, proof of signature and payment, contract notes, statements | 10 years | Date of the transaction |
| Internal alerts, enhanced reviews, suspicious transaction reports | 10 years | Date of the report or end of the relationship, whichever is later |
| Accounting books and supporting documents | 10 years | Close of the financial year |
| Articles of association, minutes of the Board and of general meetings, corporate registers | Lifetime of the Company | — |
| Contracts with clients, issuers and service providers | 10 years | End of the contract |
| Personnel and payroll files | 10 years | Departure of the Staff Member |
| Detailed log of client actions in the Guichet | 13 months, then counters only | Date of the action |

Where a document falls within several categories, the longest period applies.

### Article 6 — No more, no less

An archive is kept **at least** for its retention period, and **not beyond it** where it contains personal data, unless frozen (Article 15). Keeping data too long exposes the Company, in the event of a leak, for data it no longer needed.

### Article 7 — Versions of the document repository

Every version of a code, policy, procedure or contract template withdrawn from the document repository is kept, with its approval sheet, for ten years after it is replaced. The Company must be able to state which rule applied on any given date.

## Title III — Media and locations

### Article 8 — Electronic storage is the rule

Archives are kept **in electronic form**, in the Company's systems:

- the **Guichet**, for client files, orders, documents produced and the audit log;
- the Company's **Microsoft 365 document workspace**, for all other documents, filed in accordance with Appendix 2.

No Company document is kept in a personal mailbox, on a USB stick or on a personal computer. A document received through any other channel is transferred into the system the same day.

### Article 9 — Paper originals

Documents signed by hand are **scanned** on the day they are received, and the image is attached to the file. The paper original is kept in the **originals cabinet**, locked, in a room not exposed to water, in the custody of the Chief Executive Officer. The slip in Appendix 3 records each deposit and each removal.

A paper original is not destroyed after scanning until legal counsel has confirmed that the digital copy has the same evidential value.

### Article 10 — Integrity and evidence

For an electronic archive to serve as evidence, the Company must be able to show that it has not been altered since it was created. To that end:

- documents produced by the Guichet are time-stamped and linked to the **audit log**, each line of which carries the fingerprint of the previous one; a break in the chain is an incident to be reported;
- a signed document is never altered: a correction gives rise to a new document that refers to the first;
- the Microsoft 365 archive spaces keep the version history and prevent deletion by Staff Members other than the Chief Executive Officer;
- backups follow the Business Continuity Plan (PC-POL-PCA-001), with a copy kept outside the main provider.

### Article 11 — Access

Access to archives is limited to what each person needs for their work. AML/CFT files and suspicious transaction reports are accessible only to the RCCI and the Chief Executive Officer. Access rights are reviewed every six months, together with the review of rights provided for by the Risk Management Policy.

### Article 12 — Data location

Electronic archives are hosted by service providers, some of which are located outside Cameroon. These hosting arrangements are recorded in the outsourcing register (PC-POL-EXT-001) and handled in accordance with the transfer rules of Law No. 2024/017 on the protection of personal data.

## Title IV — Life cycle of archives

### Article 13 — Day-to-day archiving

Each archive owner files documents as they go, in the filing plan in Appendix 2. When a file is closed (end of the relationship, transaction settled, financial year closed), it is marked as archived with the starting date of its retention period.

### Article 14 — Communication to the authorities

Any request from COSUMAF, ANIF, the judicial authorities or the statutory auditor is passed without delay to the RCCI, who gathers the documents and keeps a list of them. Professional secrecy cannot be invoked against COSUMAF. A request from ANIF or a judicial requisition is never disclosed to the client concerned.

### Article 15 — Freezing of archives

No archive is destroyed, even at the end of its period, where it relates to:

- a dispute, an unresolved complaint or litigation, current or reasonably foreseeable;
- an investigation or inspection by COSUMAF, ANIF or another authority;
- a suspicious transaction report whose outcome is not known.

The RCCI records the freeze in the register in Appendix 5, informs the owners concerned, and lifts it in writing when the cause has ceased.

### Article 16 — Destruction

Each year, in the first quarter, the RCCI draws up the list of archives that have reached the end of their period and are not frozen. The Chief Executive Officer authorises their destruction. Destruction is carried out:

- for paper, by shredding, on site or by a service provider that issues a certificate;
- for electronic records, by deletion from all systems, including backups at their next rotation.

It is recorded in the minutes in Appendix 4. Clients' personal data are then erased or anonymised; counters and statistics that no longer make it possible to identify anyone may be kept.

### Article 17 — Departure of a service provider or a Staff Member

At the end of a contract with a service provider that holds archives, the Company recovers all its data in a readable format, in accordance with the reversibility clause of the Outsourcing Policy, and obtains proof of their erasure by the service provider. When a Staff Member leaves, their line manager checks that their documents are filed in the Company's spaces before their account is closed.

## Title V — Control and final provisions

### Article 18 — Controls

| Level | Who | What | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First level | Desk | Originals scanned and attached to the file the same day; slip kept up to date | At each deposit |
| Second level | RCCI | Sample of files: documents present, ability to retrieve them in less than one day | Every six months |
| Second level | RCCI | Check of the Guichet audit trail; test restoration of an archive | Every six months |
| Second level | RCCI | List of destructions and freezes | Every year |

### Article 19 — Entry into force

This procedure enters into force on the date of its approval, as indicated in the document control sheet. It is reviewed every year and whenever the archiving system changes.
