Minimum periods. At the end of the period, the archive is destroyed in accordance with Article 16, unless frozen (Appendix 5). Periods marked "to be confirmed" are subject to legal counsel's review.

| Category | Examples | Period | Starting point | Medium and location | Owner | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client files | Identity documents, supporting documents, questionnaire, review sheets, screenings, accepted agreement | 10 years | End of the relationship | Guichet; paper originals in cabinet | Desk | REG-02-24; AML/CFT Policy Art. 26 |
| Orders and transactions | Order intentions, subscription forms, proof of signature and payment, slips, SVT quotes, contract notes, statements | 10 years | Date of the transaction | Guichet | Desk | REG-02-24; PC-PRO-ORD-001 |
| AML/CFT | Internal alerts, enhanced reviews, suspicious transaction reports, exchanges with ANIF, freezes | 10 years | Report or end of the relationship, whichever is later | RCCI restricted space | RCCI | REG-02-24 |
| Log of client actions | Views and actions in the Guichet | 13 months, then counters | Date of the action | Guichet | Desk | Agreement PC-CTR-004 Art. 8 |
| Guichet audit trail | Log of desk and system actions | 10 years | Date of the action | Guichet | Chief Executive Officer | Evidence |
| Accounting | Books, financial statements, supporting documents, bank statements | 10 years | Close of the financial year | Microsoft 365; originals in cabinet | Accounting | AUDCIF (article to be confirmed) |
| Corporate records | Articles of association, minutes of the Board and general meetings, registers, COSUMAF decisions, licence | Lifetime of the Company | — | Originals cabinet and Microsoft 365 | Chief Executive Officer | AUSCGIE |
| Contracts | Agreements with issuers, service provider contracts, leases, insurance | 10 years | End of the contract | Microsoft 365; originals in cabinet | Chief Executive Officer | Commercial limitation period |
| Personnel | Employment contracts, payslips, CNPS returns, disciplinary files | 10 years (to be confirmed) | Departure of the Staff Member | Microsoft 365, restricted access | Chief Executive Officer | Labour Code; CNPS |
| Unsuccessful applications | CVs, letters | 2 years | End of the recruitment | Microsoft 365 | Chief Executive Officer | Law No. 2024/017 |
| Compliance and control | Control sheets, registers, annual reports, independent review reports | 10 years | Date of the document | RCCI space | RCCI | PC-COD-003 |
| Document repository | Withdrawn versions, approval sheets | 10 years | Replacement of the version | Repository | RCCI | Article 7 |
| Complaints | Complaint, exchanges, response | 10 years | Closure of the complaint | Guichet | Desk | Evidence |
| Crises and incidents | Crisis log, incident sheets, lessons learned, back-up order register | 10 years | End of the incident | Microsoft 365 | RCCI | PC-POL-PCA-001 |
| Routine correspondence | Emails with no evidential value | 3 years | Date sent | Microsoft 365 | Each Staff Member | Good practice |
