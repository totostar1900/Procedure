The dates are those known at the time of drafting; they are confirmed each year with the chartered accountant in the light of the General Tax Code and the finance law. Rates are not repeated here: Accounting applies those in force. The internal date is at least three business days before the deadline. Returns to COSUMAF, BVMAC and the Central Securities Depository follow the calendar of PC-PRO-REP-001.
