**Purpose Capital S.A.** — Rue Joseph Essono Balla, Elig-Essono, Yaoundé (Cameroon)

For the attention of: ______________________ (bank, SVT or account keeper)

**Subject: acknowledgement of third-party account status — account no. ______________________**

Dear Sir or Madam,

Purpose Capital S.A., a brokerage firm (société de bourse) licensed by COSUMAF under no. COSUMAF-SDB-01/2026, has opened the above account in your books, under the name "______________________".

This account is intended exclusively to receive the **cash** or **securities** belonging to the clients of Purpose Capital S.A. These assets are not the property of Purpose Capital S.A. and do not form part of its estate.

We kindly ask you to confirm, by countersigning this letter, that you acknowledge:

1. that the assets recorded in this account belong to the clients of Purpose Capital S.A.;
2. that you may not exercise over these assets any right of set-off, retention, pledge or security in respect of any debt or commitment of Purpose Capital S.A. towards you;
3. that, in the event of an attachment, third-party garnishment notice or insolvency proceedings against Purpose Capital S.A., you will inform the creditor or the court-appointed officer that this account is a third-party account, and will notify us without delay;
4. that you will send us a statement at least monthly, and a daily statement at our request.

Please return one countersigned copy to us.
