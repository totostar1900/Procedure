Indicative programme, to be adjusted each year. Recurring items at each ordinary meeting: approval of the previous minutes, follow-up of decisions, risk and internal control dashboard (PC-POL-RIS-001, PC-COD-003), overdue recommendations, significant events and relations with COSUMAF.

| Meeting | Period | Specific items | Documents to prepare | Prepared by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | March | Approval of the financial statements for the year; management report; convening of the general meeting; approval of the annual internal control report and the risk map before they are sent to COSUMAF (31 March); annual AML/CFT report; Chairman's meeting with the RCCI without Executive Management | Financial statements, statutory auditors' report, annual internal control report, risk map | Chief Executive Officer; RCCI |
| 2 | June | Budget monitoring; review of the risk appetite statement and limits; annual review of policies falling due; business continuity plan (review of exercises) | Dashboard, repository documents due for review | Chief Executive Officer; RCCI |
| 3 | September | Strategy meeting: strategic direction, new products and markets, human and IT resources, three-year plan | Strategy note, risk analysis of projects | Chief Executive Officer |
| 4 | December | Budget for the following year; annual control plan; independent review mandate; evaluation of the Chief Executive Officer; Board self-assessment; schedule for the following year | Draft budget, control plan, evaluation grid (Appendix 5) | Chief Executive Officer; RCCI; Chairman |
| Extraordinary | As needed | Change submitted to COSUMAF; major incident; major finding; COSUMAF inspection or sanction; transaction exceeding the powers of the Chief Executive Officer | Summary note | Chief Executive Officer or Chairman |
