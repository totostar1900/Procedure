## Title I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

This procedure describes what Purpose Capital S.A. (the "Company") does between the moment a client expresses an intention to invest or to sell and the moment the client receives the contract note: who does what, in what order, with which controls and which evidence.

It implements the Best Execution Policy (PC-POL-EXE-001), the Code of Conduct and the securities account opening agreement (PC-CTR-004).

### Article 2 — Scope

The procedure covers:

- **securities listed on BVMAC**: shares and bonds, for purchase and sale;
- CEMAC **government securities**: subscription at auctions and purchase or sale on the secondary market, through a primary dealer (spécialiste en valeurs du Trésor, SVT);
- **public offerings**;
- **units in collective investment undertakings**: subscription and redemption.

It applies both to orders received through the Guichet and to orders received at the branch.

### Article 3 — Who does what

| Function | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Client relationship officer | Receives intentions at the branch; informs the client; never transmits an order to the market personally |
| Desk (operations) | Confirms intentions, checks orders, transmits them, monitors execution and settlement, issues contract notes. Function performed by the Chief Executive Officer until a head of operations is appointed |
| Head of Capital Markets | Enters, or supervises the entry of, orders on the BVMAC platform; requests quotes from SVTs; decides difficult execution cases |
| RCCI | Monitors execution quality, errors and compliance with the procedure; handles AML/CFT alerts |

### Article 4 — The basic rules

1. **An intention is not an order.** It binds neither the client nor the Company, and calls for no payment.
2. **An order comes into being when the client signs it**: in their Guichet personal area (one-time code, passkey or device code), or on an order form signed at the branch.
3. **Money never precedes the order**: it accompanies or follows it, with the order reference, and comes from an account in the client's name.
4. **Only the client gives their orders**, or their representatives declared in the client file. No power of attorney is accepted.
5. **First received, first transmitted.** No order is delayed in favour of another order, nor of an order for own account or for a Staff Member.
6. **Only recorded channels** are accepted: the Guichet, signed paper order form, archived business e-mail. Personal messaging services are excluded.

## Title II — Reception of the order

### Article 5 — From intention to signed order

1. **Intention**: the client states in the Guichet, or to the client relationship officer, what they wish to do: security, direction, amount or quantity, any limit price, period of validity.
2. **Confirmation**: the desk checks the intention (Article 7) and confirms it within one business hour, or calls the client back if a point needs clarifying. The Guichet then generates the order form and, for a purchase, the call for funds.
3. **Signature**: the client signs the order form. Where the price is not known at signature, the client signs for a **maximum amount** that the Company will never exceed.
4. **Cover**: for a purchase or a subscription, the client pays immediately by an authenticated means of payment, or transfers the funds with the order reference (Article 8). For a sale, the desk checks that the securities are available and unencumbered.

The order is **time-stamped** when it is signed. It is this time-stamp that determines its ranking.

### Article 6 — Content of an order

Every order states at least: the client and their account number, the security (name and code), the direction, the quantity or amount, the order type (limit or market order) and the limit, the period of validity, and the date and time of signature. For a sale of government securities or bonds, it bears the client's certification that the securities are unencumbered. The template paper order form is set out in Appendix 2.

### Article 7 — Checks before acceptance

Before accepting an order, the desk checks, using the checklist in Appendix 1:

- that the **client file is approved and up to date**, and that the agreement in force has been accepted;
- that the client is neither blocked nor the subject of a screening alert;
- that the order is **consistent**: direction, quantity, limit, security; a manifestly erroneous order is confirmed with the client before transmission;
- that the transaction is **consistent with the client's profile**; if not, the client is informed of the mismatch, this information is recorded, and an unusual transaction is reported to the RCCI;
- for a market order on BVMAC, that the client has been informed of the risk of execution at a price far from the last traded price;
- for joint ownership (indivision), that the ceiling of FCFA 25,000,000 in nominal value is not exceeded;
- for an order that is significant in size relative to the liquidity of the security, that splitting it over several trading sessions has been proposed.

### Article 8 — Cover and funds

An order is only transmitted once it is **signed and covered**:

- funds are received in the Company's segregated settlement account, from an account in the client's name, with the order reference; a payment from a third party is refused and returned;
- the desk reconciles each payment with the order it covers; a payment without a reference is placed on hold and the client is contacted;
- the client may use the balance they have built up in their settlement account; they are never obliged to do so;
- if the funds are not received before the cut-off time for the transaction, the order lapses; the client is informed.

No cash payment is accepted.

## Title III — Transmission and execution

### Article 9 — Securities listed on BVMAC

The Company is a member of BVMAC and transmits orders to the market itself.

1. Orders signed and covered before the **internal cut-off time** set in Appendix 1 to the Best Execution Policy are entered for the same day's trading session; the others, for the next session.
2. They are entered in the **chronological order** of their time-stamp. The Head of Capital Markets, or their authorised deputy, enters the order; a second person checks the entry before the close of the pre-opening phase where staffing allows, otherwise on the same day.
3. After the session, the desk retrieves the results of the fixing: full, partial or no execution.
4. An unexecuted order remains in the order book until the end of its validity, after which the client is informed.

### Article 10 — Government securities auctions

1. The desk publishes the forthcoming auction in the Guichet, based on the issuance calendar, together with the Company's cut-off time for receiving orders.
2. Clients sign their subscription form and pay the funds before that cut-off time.
3. The desk combines the subscriptions in a **single bid schedule** addressed to the SVT, which lists each client and their amount, and transmits it before the SVT's cut-off time.
4. When the results are published, the desk allocates the securities in accordance with the auction rules and the Best Execution Policy. Unused sums become available again as soon as the result is known.
5. Each client receives a notice of allocation or non-allocation.

### Article 11 — Government securities on the secondary market

For each order, the desk requests quotes from at least two SVTs, or three for an order of significant size, compares them in terms of yield and price, including costs, and selects the best executable quote. It records the quotes received, the time and the reason for the choice on the competitive quotation sheet (Appendix 4 to the Best Execution Policy). If only one quote is available, the client is informed before execution.

### Article 12 — Public offerings

Subscriptions are transmitted to the lead manager during the subscription period, without delay or modification. If the Company is itself arranger or a member of the placement syndicate, the Conflicts of Interest Policy applies: clients' orders are never served after those of the Company or its Staff Members.

### Article 13 — Units in collective investment undertakings

The desk groups orders by management company and transmits them before the **cut-off time for centralisation** set in the fund's prospectus, for execution at the first possible net asset value. The units are registered in the client's name in the fund's register. The management company confirms the number of units and the net asset value.

### Article 14 — Aggregation and allocation

Orders from several clients in the same security and in the same direction may be transmitted together where this is not disadvantageous to any of them. In the event of partial execution, the securities are allocated **pro rata** to the quantities requested, with odd lots being allocated in chronological order. Allocation is made before the price obtained by each client is known and cannot be changed afterwards. An order for own account or for a Staff Member is never aggregated with a client order.

## Title IV — After execution

### Article 15 — Contract note

The client receives a **contract note** no later than the business day following execution: date and time, venue, security, direction, quantity, price or net asset value, accrued interest, breakdown of costs, net amount. An order that remains unexecuted at the end of its validity, or that is not filled, is the subject of a notice.

### Article 16 — Settlement and reconciliation

The desk monitors settlement with the central securities depository, the SVT or the management company. On the settlement date, it checks that the securities are registered in the client's name and that the cash has been debited or credited, and the movement is then recorded in the client's cash ledger. Each day, it reconciles orders, executions and settlements using Appendix 4. Any discrepancy is treated as an incident.

### Article 17 — Amendment and cancellation

The client may amend or cancel their order **as long as it has not been transmitted**. Once transmitted, the order is irrevocable; the desk attempts a cancellation on the market where the rules allow, without any guarantee of success. Any amendment or cancellation is time-stamped and confirmed to the client in writing.

### Article 18 — Errors

An error attributable to the Company (direction, quantity, security, account, timing) is corrected as soon as it is detected, at no cost or loss to the client. Any gain belongs to the client. The error is reported to the RCCI within 24 hours on the incident form of the Risk Management Policy, and analysed to prevent it from recurring.

### Article 19 — Complaints

A complaint about an order is acknowledged within two business days and answered within thirty days, in accordance with the complaints handling procedure.

## Title V — Control and retention

### Article 20 — Order register

The Guichet keeps the register of all orders: time-stamps of the intention, signature, cover, transmission and execution, author of each step, documents generated. Paper order forms are entered on the same day and filed. If the Guichet is unavailable, the back-up register of the Business Continuity Plan is used.

### Article 21 — Controls

| Level | Who | What | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First level | Desk | Checklist in Appendix 1 before each transmission | For each order |
| First level | Desk | Reconciliation of orders, executions and settlements (Appendix 4) | Daily |
| Second level | RCCI | Consistency between signed order, transmitted order and contract note, on a sample | Monthly |
| Second level | RCCI | Execution quality according to the scorecard in the Best Execution Policy: timeliness, chronological order, competitive quotes from SVTs, errors, complaints | Quarterly |

### Article 22 — Retention

Order forms, signatures, proofs of payment, schedules, quotes, notices and records are retained for **ten years**.

### Article 23 — Entry into force

This procedure enters into force on the date of its approval, as shown in the document control sheet. It is reviewed every year and whenever the market rules change.
