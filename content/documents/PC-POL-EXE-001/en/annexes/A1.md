Price fluctuation limits: indicative values from BVMAC Instruction No. 2, to be confirmed at each update.

| Instrument | Execution venue | Trading method | Company's access | Internal transmission cut-off time | Parameters to be communicated to the client |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listed shares | BVMAC, electronic trading platform | Daily fixing, Monday to Friday (BVMAC Notice No. 057/2023) | Market member, direct access | 30 minutes before the end of the pre-opening phase (time to be confirmed with the BVMAC) | Limit order or market order; valid for the day, until a set date or until cancelled (365 days maximum); indicative static limit ± 10% |
| Listed bonds | BVMAC, electronic trading platform | Daily fixing, price as a percentage of nominal value | Market member, direct access | Same as for shares | Indicative static limit ± 6%; accrued interest payable in addition |
| Government securities, primary market | Auctions organised with the BEAC | Multiple-price or multiple-rate auction | Through a primary dealer (spécialiste en valeurs du Trésor) | According to the published issuance calendar | Amount, limit price or rate, value date |
| Government securities, secondary market | Over the counter between primary dealers; reporting to the BVMAC unlisted (hors cote) segment | Quote on request | Competitive quotes from at least two SVTs: [SVT 1], [SVT 2], [SVT 3] | Before 11:00 for same-day execution | Yield to maturity, clean price, accrued interest, costs |
| Primary issues by public offering | Centralisation by the lead manager brokerage firm (société de bourse) | Subscription during the offer period | Direct if the Company is a member of the syndicate; otherwise by transmission to the lead manager | According to the transaction timetable | Issue price, allocation rules, settlement date |
| Units in collective investment schemes | The scheme's management company | Net asset value | Appointed distributor | Centralisation time in the prospectus | Applicable net asset value, subscription and redemption fees |
