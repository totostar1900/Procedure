**Our commitment.** When we receive, transmit or execute your orders, we take all sufficient steps to obtain the best possible result for you. For an individual client, the main criterion is the total cost of the transaction: the price of the instrument and all costs.

**Where your orders are executed.**

- Listed shares and bonds: on the BVMAC, the only listing market in the CEMAC zone, at the daily fixing from Monday to Friday.
- Government securities bought or sold before maturity: with primary dealers (spécialistes en valeurs du Trésor), whom we put in competition to obtain the best quote.
- Subscriptions to a new issue or to a collective investment scheme: on the terms set by the issuer or the management company.

**What we do for you.**

- Your order is time-stamped as soon as it is received and transmitted in order of arrival, and is never delayed in favour of another client, our Company or our Staff Members.
- We recommend a limit order, which protects your price in a market where some securities trade infrequently. If you choose a market order, we inform you of the risk of execution at a price far from the last price.
- For a large order, we may suggest executing it over several sessions to avoid moving the price to your detriment.
- You receive a trade confirmation no later than the business day following execution, with details of the price and costs.
- Any error on our part is corrected at no cost or loss to you.

**Your instructions and our control.** If you give us a specific instruction (price, timing, terms), we follow it, even if it may prevent us from applying some of the measures above. Our compliance function checks execution quality every quarter; on simple request, we will demonstrate to you that your orders have been executed in accordance with our policy.
