**Template 1 — Disruption** (SMS or email)

> Purpose Capital: due to [brief cause], our services have been disrupted since [time]. Your assets are not affected. To place an order or for any question, call [backup number]. We will let you know when services are back to normal.

**Template 2 — Return to normal**

> Purpose Capital: our services have been operating normally again since [time]. Orders received during the disruption have been processed; you are receiving confirmation of them. Thank you for your patience.

**Template 3 — Interruption of a market infrastructure**

> Purpose Capital: [BVMAC / the Central Securities Depository] has suspended [trading / settlements] since [time]. Your orders are retained and will be transmitted as soon as service resumes, unless you cancel them. Contact: [number].

**Template 4 — Permanent cessation of business** (to be approved by COSUMAF before sending)

> Purpose Capital: the Company is no longer able to continue its business as from [date]. Your securities remain recorded in your name with [Central Securities Depository / account keeper] and your funds are held in a separate account with [bank]. To recover them or transfer them to another intermediary, [instructions]. You may also contact COSUMAF.
